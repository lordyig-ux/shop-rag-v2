# Legal and regulatory requirements for towing suppliers

Requirements that involve all applicable laws and regulations governing towing vehicles and operators.

### General requirements

Towing vehicles and operators must comply with all applicable laws and regulations. These include (but are not limited to) all laws and regulations that govern

* ownership and operation of motor vehicles
* environmental protection, and
* occupational health and safety.
