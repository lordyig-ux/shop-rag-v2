# Handle faulty workmanship

Steps to handle faulty workmanship on a vehicle and then involving an Estimating Services manager to assist in the estimate.

### When this applies

You have determined that faulty workmanship may be the cause of loss.

### Procedure

1. Proceed with the faulty workmanship as follows:

   | If the repair facility is... | Then... |
   | --- | --- |
   | a collision repair program participant | * refer the claim to the Manager Estimating Services, and * notify the Accreditation department. |
   | a non-participating collision repair facility | refer the claim to the Manager Estimating Services. |
2. Complete task.
