# Warranties for recycled parts

List of warranty types for recycled parts.

### Warranty types

This table presents the various warranty types offered by authorized parts suppliers on recycled parts for ICBC claims.

| Recycled part | Warranty |
| --- | --- |
| Recycled body parts for vehicles 10 years old and newer | * Limited lifetime warranty against premature rust perforation as compared to the rest of the vehicle. * Warranty is for the original owner of the vehicle at time of repair against installation of the recycled parts. |
| Steering knuckles and control arms | * Warranty is no longer effective once the steering knuckles and control arms are installed, and * wheel alignment and road test has been completed.   Note: Wearable items attached such as wheel bearings or ball joints are not included. |
| Heated rear glass | * No warranty. * Installer must perform a second check to ensure proper operation of the heating elements prior to installation. * Recycled part supplier is not responsible for failure, accident, misuse, abuse, or improper maintenance of any part, and failure beyond the applicable warranty period. |
| All other recycled parts | * Warranty for a period of 183 days (six months) or 10,000 kilometers of the part, whichever comes first from the date of purchase. * Recycled part supplier   + replaces the part within one business day of being notified of the failure, or   + refunds the purchase price of that part, including freight for the part within the applicable warranty period.   Note: For further information refer to the topic, [Compensation for labour on recycled parts](gb35c5db-2a76-471b-89e5-251b69ed4faf.html "Summary of recycled parts that are compensated for labour."). |
