# Partial theft estimating requirements

Requirements for the customer and estimator to have for a partial theft claim.

### Customer requirements

A customer who submits a claim for equipment that has been stolen must substantiate the age, make, and model of the stolen articles. The customer must produce the following:

* invoices, and/or
* other supporting documentation, e.g. a bill of sale.

### Estimator requirements

An estimator must do the following while working on a claim involving stolen equipment

* retain original customer invoices and/or supporting documentation on claim
* inspect the vehicle to ensure the physical evidence is consistent with the equipment claimed (e.g., wiring harness, brackets, speaker holes, etc.)
* pre-price the Estimate or Appraisal using the most competitive source (e.g., recycled, aftermarket, or most competitive supplier)
* investigate the theft claim thoroughly and document the information in ClaimCenter, and
* if subrogation exists, refer the claim to the claim owner or applicable exposure owner.
