# Replace a lost Estimate/Appraisal form

How to assist a customer who loses their Estimate or Appraisal form.

### When this applies

At times, customers lose their Estimate or Appraisal form. If a customer loses their estimate form, then assist with replacing the lost form.

Vehicle repairs can still take place, as long as they are completed within the limitation period.

### Procedure

1. Inform the customer that vehicle repairs can still take place provided the repair takes place within the limitation period.
2. Send a copy of the Estimate or Appraisal form to the customer via

   * email
   * fax, or
   * mail.
3. Disengage the customer, and determine if any additional enquiries need to be addressed.

   | If … | Then … |
   | --- | --- |
   | yes | address each enquiry |
   | no | * make the closing comments, and * disconnect the call. |

5. Perform the follow-up duties which may include:

   * mailing the pertinent documents to the customer, or
   * advising an interested party of the customer’s call.
