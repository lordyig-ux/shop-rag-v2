# Perform biohazard inspection and cleaning

Steps on performing biohazard inspections and cleaning of a vehicle.

### Prerequisites

A vehicle was exposed to contaminated fluids.

### When this applies

Inspection and cleaning of vehicles with potential biohazard contamination is performed by qualified service providers.

After taking appropriate safety precautions you are ready to inspect and then clean the vehicle.

### Procedure

1. Complete a thorough inspection of the entire passenger
   area of the vehicle, inspecting all compartments under, over, in and around the interior
   of the vehicle, including the trunk.
2. Bag and tag the customer’s personal property.

   | If the vehicle is ... | Then ... |
   | --- | --- |
   | repairable | leave the bagged and tagged items in the vehicle. |
   | a total loss | bring the bagged and tagged items to the attention of the estimator. |
3. Clean the entire vehicle, or required areas of the vehicle.
4. Hang a Mirror Hanger card (GEN284) from the rear-view mirror displaying the 

   * company who cleaned the vehicle, their business card, and
   * estimator’s business card.

   Note: All vehicles that have been inspected and/or cleaned require a Mirror Hanger card (GEN284).
