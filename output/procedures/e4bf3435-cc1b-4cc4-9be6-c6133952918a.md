# Electronic handling fees

List of electronic handling fees that ICBC suppliers pay when they purchase specified products for the customer’s vehicle repairs.

### Fees

ICBC suppliers do not pay the electronic handling fees on Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) supplied electronic equipment.

Electronic handling fees for purchased products are in the following table:

| Specified Products | Electronic fees | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| After-market vehicle audio, and video systems | $2.75 per product | * In-dash compact disk (CD) and/or cassette players * amplifiers * equalizers * speakers * video player systems, and * video displays, including those with built in tuners. |
| Display devices in a recreational vehicle | $9.00 per product per 29 inch screen or smaller  $31.75 per product per 30 inch screen or greater | * Television * computer monitor * professional display, and * television with built in digital video recorder (DVD) and/or video cassette recorders (VCR) player and recorder. |
| Home audio, video playback and/or recording systems in a recreational vehicle | $3.50 per product | * AM/FM radio * VCR * digital video recorder (DVR) * personal video recorder (PVR) * digital compact disc players or recorders (DVD, Blu-ray) * laser disc players or recorders * cable and satellite receivers, and * set top boxes, including digital television. |
| Home stereo systems or components in a recreational vehicle | $3.50 per product | * Amplifiers * receivers * CD or tape deck * turntables or record players * home speaker systems (5.1 and 7.1 speaker packages supplied without amplifier or video player, including computer multi-media speakers) * other digital music recorders or players * audio docking stations * analog and digital video cameras for home security or other closed circuit home use, and * video baby monitor systems. |
