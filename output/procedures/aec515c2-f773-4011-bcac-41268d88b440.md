# Apply commercial parts and sublet mark-up rates

Steps for dealerships and independent suppliers to apply parts and sublet mark-up rates on commercial vehicles at their facility.

### When this applies

Commercial vehicle with an ICBC claim is at a dealership or independent supplier for repair.

### Procedure

1. Apply the appropriate commercial parts and sublet mark-up rate to the claim.

   | If the … | Then … |
   | --- | --- |
   | dealership repairs vehicles from a different manufacturer, such as a Freightliner repairing a Kenworth. | * they can mark-up parts purchased as per the applicable rate in the [MD Rate Schedule](https://www.icbc.com/partners/material-damage/Pages/rate-schedule.aspx) . * the cost of the non-captive or used part (including the mark-up) must not exceed the dealership captive part Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM), and * in the MD Rate Schedule are the:   + non-captive parts mark-up over cost   + used parts mark-up over cost, and   + sublet mark-up over cost. |
   | independent supplier repairs vehicles from a manufacturer | * cost of the non-captive or used part (including the dealership’s mark-up) must not exceed the dealership captive part (OEM), and * sublet mark-up over cost is in the MD Rate Schedule. |
2. Complete task.
