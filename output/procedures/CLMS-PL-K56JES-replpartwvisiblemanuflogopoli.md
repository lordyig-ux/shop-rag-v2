# Parts with a visible manufacturer’s logo replacement policy

ICBC is responsible for covering the cost of repairing or replacing the vehicle, and its equipment or any part of it, with material of a similar kind or quality. When determining if an aftermarket part is an appropriate replacement for parts that have a visible Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) logo, the overall aesthetic appearance of the part must be considered.

### Applicability

The decision on the type of part to use, OEM versus aftermarket, is based on the impact that the logo has to the overall aesthetic of the vehicle.

| Effect on aesthetic of vehicle | Part to use | Visual representation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Logo does not improve overall aesthetic of vehicle and is not visible unless viewing up close | Aftermarket part, when available |  |
| Logo is clearly for aesthetic appearance | OEM part |  |

### Exceptions

OEM parts must be utilized if the vehicle has one of the following special endorsement coverages in place:

* Replacement Cost Endorsement (APV286A), or
* New Vehicle Replacement Plus (NVR+).

Note: For more information, refer to the topic, [Effect of special endorsements on collision repair program facilities](Topic.aspx?rootmap=DAMG-MP-NRP91J-damage&topic=192734C620D77F2DDA7FA67E4DE68D0E "Summary of impacts of special endorsements on collision repair program facilities").
