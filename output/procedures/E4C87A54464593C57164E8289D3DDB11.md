# Environmental commitment policy

ICBC has environmental commitments to meet.

### Applicability

ICBC meets its environmental commitments by

* complying with applicable environmental laws and industry practices, and expects the same of its contractors
* identifying and managing environmental risks to reduce the environmental impact of its operations
* integrating environmental protection measures into its business activities
* committing to being prepared for environmental emergencies
* using energy and materials wisely to conserve natural resources and reduce waste
* training all employees on their responsibility to protect the environment, and
* monitoring its activities and continually improves its environmental performance.

Important: Refer to ICBC's [Environmental Sustainability policy](https://thehub/manuals/CompanyPolicies/Business-Operations/Pages/EnvironmentalSustainability.aspx).
