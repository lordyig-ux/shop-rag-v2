# Obsolete parts policy

Policy on obsolete parts and ICBC’s liability.

### Limit of liability

Where there is loss or damage to

* an obsolete part of an insured vehicle, or
* a part the manufacturer does not have in stock

ICBC’s liability is limited to the price at which the part was last listed on the manufacturer’s price list.

Note: Refer to ICBC Autoplan Optional Policy - Division 8, Prescribed Condition 5(8).
