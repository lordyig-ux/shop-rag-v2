# Communication with suppliers

Guidelines on communicating with suppliers.

### Communication

ICBC encourages open dialogue with the repair and service industry for the benefit of our customers.

To ensure open communication with suppliers, ICBC representatives are available to

* attend, assist or conduct meetings or seminars to enhance industry relations and knowledge, and/or
* assist in resolving outstanding issues.
