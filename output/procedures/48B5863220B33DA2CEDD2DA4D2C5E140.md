# Repair facility move allowance

Situations when repair facility moves are allowed with commercial or heavy equipment vehicles.

### When facility moves are allowed

Sometimes, a commercial or heavy equipment vehicle is being repaired and requires movement within the facility to assist with the repair process.

Facility moves are considered only

* when a commercial or heavy equipment vehicle can be moved only with a tow truck or facility lift vehicle, due to extensive damage such as missing suspension components, and
* to facilitate the required repairs, such as

  + moving the vehicle to frame rack, or
  + moving a non-driveable vehicle from the yard into the facility.

### Allowance

Allowance is as follows:

| When the tow is completed... | Then applicable allowance is... |
| --- | --- |
| In- house | 1.5 hours labour. |
| by an external tow company | the cost of the tow (with no mark up). |
