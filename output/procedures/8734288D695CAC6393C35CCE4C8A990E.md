# Aftermarket tinting rules

Aftermarket tinting must be reviewed to confirm it adheres to legislated requirements.

Aftermarket glass tinting is allowed only on:

* the top 75 mm of the windshield
* a side window that is behind the driver, or
* the rear window if the vehicle is equipped with outside rear view mirrors on both the left and right sides.

### Applicable legislation/policy

Motor Vehicle Act Regulation - Section 7.05 (8).
