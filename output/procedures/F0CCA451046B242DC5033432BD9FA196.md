# ICBC supplier form requirements

Form requirements to qualify as an ICBC supplier.

### Supplier forms

Suppliers must complete specific forms to become an ICBC approved supplier.

When a new supplier is added, suspended, or has a change of status of an existing supplier to SAP Vendor Master, there are forms that must be completed and approved according to the service type and supplier.

The table presents the types of forms, supplier and approver that must be completed for a new ICBC supplier:

| Form | Supplier | Approver |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New/Change Supplier application – Trade (CL174) form | Material Damage supplier | Manager, Accreditation Governance and Supplier |
| New/Change Supplier application – Professional (CL174D) form | * Independent adjusters * Private investigators * Engineers | Injury Technical and Program Support Advisor |
| CL174D form | Legal firms | Special Counsel, Legal Services |
| New/Change BI Service Provider Supplier (CL174G) form | Bodily injury rehabilitation service providers | Manager, Administration Rehabilitation Operations |
| CL174G form | Massage therapist | Injury Technical and Support Advisor |
