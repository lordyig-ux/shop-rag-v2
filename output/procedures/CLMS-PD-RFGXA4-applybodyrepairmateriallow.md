# Add the additional materials allowance to an estimate

The repair program participant applies the additional materials allowance for the cost of materials or parts used during repairs.

### When this applies

When additional materials or parts are required during the repair process, the additional materials allowance is entered through Mitchell Cloud Estimating (MCE).

Note: For more information on materials covered under the allowance, refer to [Body repair material allowances](0116D11369C1EDE81581925D8CB1BC7F.html "List of body repair materials included in the standard hourly allowance and miscellaneous parts that are charged as individual parts.").

### Procedure

1. Navigate to the estimate and click the Parts icon in the side bar.
2. Select: My Templates > 01 Standard.
3. Navigate to 01 Standard menu.
4. Select: Additional Materials and add line.
5. Enter the total units and total price before taxes.
6. Attach an explanation line note to the Templates line.

   **Explanation line note example**: Lt Quarter replacement required 1.5 cartridges of panel adhesive

   Note: Requires Review is not required for additional material allowances. The quantity of materials or parts entered on the estimate must reflect what was required to complete the repair.
