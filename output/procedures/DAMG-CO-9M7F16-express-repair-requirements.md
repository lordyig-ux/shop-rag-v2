# Collision repair requirements

Some requirements that must be met for a vehicle to automatically qualify for the collision repair program.

### Claim requirements

In order to qualify for collision repair, refer to the claim requirements and applicable circumstance.

| Requirement | Circumstance |
| --- | --- |
| ICBC coverage | The customer must have purchased Collision or Comprehensive coverage from ICBC. |
| Rate class | The vehicle must be insured in one of the following rate classes:  * 001 * 002 * 003 * 004 * 007 * 011 * 012 * 014 * 015 |
| Type of claim | The loss must be a:  * Collision claim, or * Comprehensive claim with body or paint damage or both due to:   + animal impact   + vandalism   + theft from auto, only if the items stolen are not insured by ICBC   + other comprehensive claims, including but not limited to lightning, hail, flying rocks, or   + unidentified ( hit and run ). |
| Vehicle condition | The vehicle must be driveable. |
| Suspicion | The claim must not be suspicious. |

### Exception

When the first party has no ICBC Collision coverage, the customer may take their vehicle to a collision repair facility only if the

* customer has chosen an collision repair facility, and
* Third party has reported, has ICBC insurance and accepts the responsibility.
