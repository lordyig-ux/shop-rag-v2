# Tow rotation list

Definition of a tow rotation list.

### Definition

A tow rotation list contains the required information that enables the assignment of non-captive tows to tow companies.

A tow rotation list:

* lists the tow companies that are within an ICBC claim centre service area, and
* is created and regularly maintained by the Supplier Programs & Administration department.

### Tow rotation list use

A tow rotation list is used to:

* track the tow companies that are within an ICBC claim centre service area, and
* aid tow administrators in selecting a tow company to perform a non-captive vehicle tow.

### Tow rotation list example

Each tow company with a valid ICBC supplier number is entered on the claim centre tow rotation list according the total number of tow vehicles that are registered to a specific ICBC claim centre and/or community.
