# Tire advance disposal fees and tire types

List of tire advance disposal fees and tire type categories.

### Fees and categories

Estimators and repair suppliers must pay the tire advance disposal fees based on the tire types.

Note: The rates are current as of January 1, 2024.

| Tire type | Definition | Fees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Passenger tires | Tires used on   * passengers cars * light trucks * multi-purpose passenger vehicles (MPV) * sport utility vehicles (SUV) * cross-over utility vehicles (CUV), and * tires that comply with the Canadian Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (CMVSS No. 109). | $6.50 plus applicable taxes. |
| Light truck tires | Tires that  * are used on consumer or commercial light trucks under 10,000 lbs gross vehicle weight (GVW), and * comply with Canadian Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (CMVSS No.119). | $6.50 plus applicable taxes. |
| Motorcycle and all terrains vehicle (ATV) | All tires used for   * on/and off highways motorcycles * motorcycles sidecars * motor bikes * mopeds * mini-cycles, and * ATVs. | $6.50 plus applicable taxes. |
| Free rolling farm | Tires used for   * free rolling farm, and * tires deemed for use on farm equipment. | $6.50 plus applicable taxes. |
| Medium truck tires, commercial truck, and bus tires | Wide base or heavy truck tires that are used for truck and bus applications, and  Recreational vehicle (RV) tires that are not marked Light Truck (LT).  Note: All comply with Canadian Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (CMVSS No. 119). | $14.00 plus applicable taxes. |
| Agricultural drive tires | Drive wheel tires used on tractors, and combine equipment.  Note: These tires are identified with a sidewall marking with suffix letters R or HF. | $15.00 plus applicable taxes. |
| Logger/skidder tires | Tires used on tree harvesting equipment, and identified with a sidewall marking with suffix letters LS. | $35.00 plus applicable taxes. |
