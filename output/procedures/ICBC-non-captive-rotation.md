# ICBC Non-captive tow rotation list

Information regarding non-captive tow rotations lists.

### Non-captive rotation

Non-captive tows are distributed by a claim centre or ICBC Salvage facility.

| Facility distributed from is ... | Non-captive tow is ... |
| --- | --- |
| a claim centre | distributed in order of rotation list:  * based on the percentage of the individual tow company's vehicle count, and * compared to the total vehicle count of all tow vehicles in the tow company's facilities to the associated claim centre. |
| an ICBC Salvage facility | distributed in order of rotation list:  * based on the percentage of the individual tow company's vehicle count, and * which tow company's facilities have main storage facilities within 30 km from an ICBC Salvage facility. |

Important: ICBC references the claim centre non-captive tow rotation list for the geographical area.
