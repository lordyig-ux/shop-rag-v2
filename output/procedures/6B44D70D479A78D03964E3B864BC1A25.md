# Replacement policy on mobile phones

Summary of replacement policy on mobile phones.

### Mobile phones covered and not covered

| Type of mobile phone design | Coverage |
| --- | --- |
| * Designed to be used independently of the vehicle, and * Not an OEM supplied part. | Not covered |
| * Not designed to be used independently of the vehicle, or * Is an OEM supplied part. | Covered |

A mobile phone must qualify as equipment of the vehicle for ICBC to replace it.

### Promotional package coverage

When replacing a qualifying mobile phone that a customer received at a discount as part of a promotional package available only when the phone is activated, ICBC pays the most competitive replacement cost of the phone unit, within coverage.

### Applicable regulation and legislation

These policies are based on the definition of equipment under

* Division 
  5, Section 5.11 (e) of the ICBC Autoplan Optional Policy, or
* Section 113 of the Revised Regulation (1984) Under the Insurance (Motor Vehicle) Act.
