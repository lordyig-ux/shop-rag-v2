# Vehicle disposition notification

Information regarding the use of the Vehicle Disposition Notification (APV377) form.

### Vehicle disposition notification form

ICBC has agreed to, in alignment with Canadian Council Motor Transport Authority (CCMTA) guidelines, report any out-of-province vehicles considered a total loss to the issuing jurisdiction’s vehicle registry.

A [Vehicle Disposition Notification (APV377)](http://icbcwebapps/corpforms/formsrc/APV377.asp) form must be completed to contact an out of province vehicle registry, when a vehicle registered outside of BC is involved in a crash with a responsible ICBC insured vehicle operating outside BC, and is determined to be a total loss.

The out of province vehicle can be from:

* another Canadian province or territory, or
* an American state.

Note: ICBC contacts the issuing registry as long as the vehicle meets salvage designation criteria, even if a cash settlement has paid on the total loss.
