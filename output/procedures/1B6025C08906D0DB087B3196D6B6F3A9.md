# Radiators and air conditioning parts requirements

Requirements for radiators and air conditioning parts.

### Requirements

Radiators and air conditioning parts must meet all these standards:

| Requirement | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Similar kind and quality | To ensure equivalent cooling efficiency, all radiator and air conditioning condensers must be equivalent to Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) in  * fit * form, and * structural design. |
| Freight | All radiator and air conditioning parts contained in the Mitchell database must include freight. This means all freight must be prepaid by the supplier within the province of British Columbia. |
| Pricing | * All radiator and air conditioning parts must be priced less than OEM for inclusion in the Mitchell database. * New core replacement (exchange radiator) pricing   + includes all labour charges for removal and installation of tanks, but   + does not include any additional parts costs for tank replacement(s) if necessary. |
| Availability | All radiator and air conditioning parts contained in the Mitchell database must be readily available within two working days. |
