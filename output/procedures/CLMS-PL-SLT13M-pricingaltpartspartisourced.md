# Pricing for participant sourced alternate parts policy

A repair program participant can bill at competitive retail pricing, not exceeding 75% of the original equipment manufacturer (OEM) MSRP, for alternate parts sourced outside of the pre-priced parts listed within the estimating platform.

Repair participants must meet requirements set out in [Standards of performance on aftermarket parts](01F6C423FBB29DEE6AAF2568694863E5.html "Repair facilities must meet all the standards of performance when dealing with aftermarket parts.").

These include but are not limited to parts such as, upper/lower control arms, tie end rods, O2 sensors, mufflers, and catalytic converters.

Refer to the topic, [Part mark-up policy](e644758c-c206-4ae1-bf19-febaa7a87abe.html "When ICBC authorizes a part mark-up on a Estimate or Appraisal form."), for more information on parts.

Note: If delivery delays impact Loss of Use (LOU) and Alternative Transportation Service (ATS), potentially charging above the pricing limits is acceptable in this instance, if it is accompanied by a line remark supporting the decision.
