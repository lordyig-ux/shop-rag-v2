# Attached part allowances for antennae and dash cameras attached to glass

List of attached part allowance for antennae and dash camera.

### Allowances

ICBC pays an allowance for removing and re-installing antennae or a dash camera attached to glass that needs replacing.

When an antenna or dash camera is attached to a glass part that requires replacement, or removal and installation, an allowance is applicable. Allowances are inclusive of part(s) replacement.

Important: If both an antennae and a dash camera are present, one of the items can be selected using the Glass Web Express (GWE) check box for "Remote Starter Antenna" and the other item must be added in GWE as an exception at the correct allowance.

| Antenna type | Allowance |
| --- | --- |
| Exterior antenna (e.g. cellular antenna, satellite antenna) | $24.95 |
| Remote starter antenna or dash camera | $15.00 |

Note: Allowance is only paid if

* supporting photos are provided, and
* the installation is covered by the facility.
