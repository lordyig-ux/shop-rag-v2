# Tow the vehicle

Steps to determine the identification of the tow type, destination, and company.

### When this applies

Vehicle tow management is a multi-step procedure that involves the identification of the tow type, destination, and company.

Non-driveable vehicles require tows to various destinations throughout the claims process, in order to

* complete the Estimate or Appraisal
* be repaired, or
* sent to the salvage facility.

### Procedure

1. Identify current vehicle location.
2. Identify the tow destination.

   Determine whether the vehicle is to be towed to

   * an ICBC facility
   * a repair facility
   * a customer’s residence, or
   * a salvage facility.
3. Identify the tow company.

   If the tow type is non-captive, then select a tow company from the claims office tow rotation list.
4. Provide tow instructions to the tow company.

   If the vehicle tow involves

   * complex recovery
   * handling a gas or fire damaged vehicle, or
   * moving a vehicle
     + more than 160 kilometers, or
     + over water

   then manage the tow handling exception.
5. Advise the repair facility of the vehicle arrival, if the vehicle destination is a collision repair facility.

   The Claims Support Assistant may contact the collision repair facility or separately email them a copy of the External Tow Dispatch web form after it has been saved to the claim.
