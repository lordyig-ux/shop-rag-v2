# Completing an electronic signature for a claim estimate

Information on how an electronic signature for a claim estimate for glass or collision is obtained from a customer.

### Prerequisites

* Repairs are completed.
* Customer portion has been paid.

### When this applies

When the customer is required to provide an electronic signature on a claim. For more information, refer to the topic, Policy on digital signatures for claim estimates.

### Process

1. **Repair facility representative**
   1. Completes the CL14/Cl14BX form.
   2. Scans and sends an electronic copy of the CL14/CL14BX to the customer via email, upon their consent.
2. **Customer**
   1. Provides authorization through

      * electronic signature, or
      * proof of signature, including wet ink signatures that are scanned or photographed and emailed to the participant.
3. **Repair facility representative**
   1. Receives the customer's electronic signature.
   2. Retains the electronic signature.
