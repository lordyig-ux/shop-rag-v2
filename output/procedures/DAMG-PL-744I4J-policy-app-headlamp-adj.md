# Approval of headlamp aim or adjustment policy

Policy on when ICBC authorizes a headlamp aim or adjustment on an Estimate/Approval form.

### Policy

The table outlines the criteria to be met in order to approve a headlamp aim or adjustment on a Estimate/Appraisal form.

| If the headlamp is … | Then … |
| --- | --- |
| replaced | approve the aim/adjustment |
| removed and installed (RE & I) to assist in the repair or replacement of a part in a headlamp mounting area |
| RE & I to assist in refinishing operations where no damage exists to the headlamp mounting area | do not approve the aim/adjustment Note: The adjustment can be approved if Mitchell auto-populates the requirement. |
| RE & I to assist in the repair or replacement of a part where the headlamp mounting area is not damaged |

Note: Situations not listed in the table must be approved by an ICBC estimator.
