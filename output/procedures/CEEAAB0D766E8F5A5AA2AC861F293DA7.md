# Crushing vehicles policy

When a vehicle is deemed a total loss due to the result of flood damage, then the vehicle is designated as non-repairable, for dismantle purposes only (DPO) or crush.

### Applicability

The policy applies to the following situations that result in a total loss, and the vehicle being designated as non-repairable.

| Situation | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Vehicle meets the description of flood damage | The vehicle must be designated  * non-repairable * flood * dismantle purposes only (DPO), or * crush.  Components of a flood damaged vehicle may not be used as donor parts for repairing or rebuilding other vehicles, unless the donor part is  * determined to be unaffected by the flooding occurrence, or * cleaned and tested, to ensure no toxic hazard or contamination remains. |
| The vehicle is  * contaminated by a toxic fluid and rendered unsafe due to toxic hazard, or * exposed to contaminated water such as salt water, fertilizer, sewage, or chemical or heavy particulate. | The vehicle must be  * designated non-repairable * flood * sold by Salvage Operations, and * crushed. |

### Exceptions

A vehicle may be exempt from receiving non-repairable flood status when

* none of the vehicle’s major electrical system components have been immersed in, or covered by liquid
* the vehicle has limited and basic electrical components available, due to the vehicle’s collectible, that is manufactured in 1971 or earlier, value, or
* the vehicle
  + was subjected to fresh water flooding without the presence of harmful or toxic contaminants, and
  + may be safely repaired.

Important: All exceptions must be approved by the salvage operations manager or operations estimating services manager, and documented in the customer's claim.
