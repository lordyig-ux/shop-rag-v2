# Loss of Use priority of payment

A priority of payment list for Loss of Use (LOU) when a customer has access to multiple sources of LOU coverage, such as roadside plus and Basic Vehicle Damage coverage.

### Priority of payment

LOU coverage is accessed based on a priority sequence when the customer has multiple sources of LOU coverage available.

Note: A customer cannot access other sources until the initial priority limits are exhausted. This is a general rule as it progresses down the priority list.

Table 1.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| First priority | * RoadStar package, or * Roadside Plus package. |
| Second priority | Optional Loss of Use |
| Third priority | Loss of Use due to theft |
| Fourth priority | Basic Vehicle Damage coverage |
| Fifth priority | The responsible party's Third Party Liability (TPL) coverage |

### LOU limit per day

Customers who have more than one source of LOU cannot stack coverages for a greater daily limit.

* Cannot stack LOU sources for greater limit per day, and
* exposure owner must ensure rental vehicle daily cost does not exceed limit per day of each LOU source.

### LOU total limit

* In most cases, customers who have more than one source of LOU can combine coverages to extend their total limit.

### Garage LOU limit per day and total limit exceptions

When a garage service operator (GSO) is responsible for damaging a customer's vehicle, the customer has access to the garage's LOU coverage, which has a

* maximum limit of $2000, and
* daily limit of $100.

These limits cannot be combined or stacked with a customer's own LOU limits.

Note: For more information, refer to the topic [Garage policy](Topic.aspx?rootmap=DAMG-MP-NRP91J-damage&topic=BF68B20E246543A16F460FCAC2C123B6 "Garage service operators (GSO) are required to purchase the , which provides basic coverage for both their owned vehicles, and customer vehicles in their care, custody, or control.").

### LOU due to theft limit per day exception

The priority of coverage can be modified for customers whose vehicle is stolen and LOU coverage has a daily limit of less than $40. In this case, the priority may be modified in order for the customer being able to use a higher daily limit first.
