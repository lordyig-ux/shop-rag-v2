# Commercial refinish times for additional applications

List of commercial refinish times for additional applications.

### Included items

Refinishing often involves additional applications. A labour time applies to each different application.

Additional applications include

* clear coat
* two tone
* cab inners, and
* commercial tint.

Refer to the [ICBC Heavy Equipment (Truck) Paint Chart](https://www.icbc.com/partners/material-damage/Documents/heavy-equipment-paint-chart.pdf) for applicable refinish times.
