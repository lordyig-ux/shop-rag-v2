# Mounting and balancing wheels

Policy on mounting and balancing wheels in the Mitchell Estimating (ME) system.

### Policy

When replacing the wheels on a vehicle in an estimate, the Mitchell Cloud Estimating (MCE) times include the removal and install (R&I) of the wheel assembly to the vehicle.

The MCE sublet pre-store for wheel balance must be entered on the estimate when a tire is removed or separated from the wheel.
