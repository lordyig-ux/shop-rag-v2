# Sublet services

Instances where a towing company requires the services of a sublet company.

### When this applies

A tow company may require additional equipment and/or services from a sublet service company to complete the recovery and transport of a vehicle.

### Examples

A sublet service company may be required for:

* flagging services (that have not been provided by a tow company directly)
* scuba diving services, and/or
* specialized rental equipment.
