# ICBC proprietary marks usage policy

ICBC has sole discretion in the use of its proprietary marks.

### Policy

ICBC’s proprietary marks are for the exclusive use of ICBC itself. ICBC has sole discretion in allowing other organizations to use the marks.

The provision of a supplier number is not:

* an authorization to use
  + ICBC’s proprietary marks
  + the term approved, or
* a certification or approval by the Corporation of the supplier’s business.

### Consequences of misuse

Any misuse of these marks and terms may result in denial

* or revocation of a supplier number, or
* of participation in the collision repair program or glass repair program.
