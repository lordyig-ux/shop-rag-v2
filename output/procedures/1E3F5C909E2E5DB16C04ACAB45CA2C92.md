# Commercial refinish times on applying integrals

List of Commercial refinish times on applying integrals or section of integrals.

### Included items

A refinish labour time applies to each integral or section of integral.

Integrals include

* complete integral
* complete integral high roof
* integral side
* integral roof, and
* high top - roof.

Refer to the [ICBC Heavy Equipment (Truck) Paint Chart](https://www.icbc.com/partners/material-damage/Documents/heavy-equipment-paint-chart.pdf) for applicable refinish times.
