# Supplier surcharges policy

Policy on suppliers submitting surcharges or additional charges to ICBC for the vehicle repairs.

### Policy

ICBC suppliers must charge for repairs based on the rates and times on the Estimate or Appraisal. Surcharging or additional charges to ICBC are not allowed.

### Eligibility

The policy applies to all ICBC suppliers where any ICBC Estimate or Appraisal form is created and authorized.

### Rationale

The policy ensures that ICBC customers do not pay more than is required to have their vehicle repaired, unless the customer has requested an upgrade or substitution.

Note: Refer to the topic, [Policy on upgrade or substitution for glass](F813468EF60CC4C932BAAEB916EFA418.html "Policy on equipment upgrades or substitutions on ICBC glass only claims."), for more information on how this affects upgrades or substitutions for glass.
