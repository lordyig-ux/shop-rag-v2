# In-stock decals and stripes and the collision repair program

The collision repair program facility follows requirements for the in-stock decals and stripes it uses.

### Claim requirements and entitlements

When a collision repair program facility uses stripes or decals from an Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) stripe or decal kit already in their inventory

* the repair facility is entitled to a price equivalent to 25% of the current OEM list price for that kit, per claim
* the image documentation requirement is the same as for any other in-stock part, and
* no other supporting documentation is required.
