# Standards of performance for repair facilities on exchange parts

Guidelines for standards of performance on exchange parts for repair facilities.

### Standards of performance

All repair facilities must meet all these standards of performance for exchange parts:

| Requirement | Standard of performance |
| --- | --- |
| In house repairs | Repair facilities must not send parts to exchange parts suppliers for repairs when a repair time is shown on the Estimate or Appraisal. These repairs must be completed in house by the repair facility. |
| Change from replace to repair | If a repair facility decides to repair a part when ICBC has specified exchange:  * the part price must be converted to a repair allowance. * the change must be:   + approved by ICBC, and   + for a negotiated repair allowance that is not to exceed the actual cost of replacement. |
| Core storage | Exchange part cores must be safely stored by the repair facility for at least 7 days, unless other arrangements have been made between the repair facility and supplier. |
| Labour compensation | Repair facility requests for labour compensation must be reasonable. |
