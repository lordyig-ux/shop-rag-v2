# Customer referrals to ICBC

Collision repair program participants refer customers to ICBC in specific situations.

### When to refer a customer to ICBC

The collision repair program participant refers a customer in specific situations.

| Situation | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Customer raises a concern about  * amount of depreciation determined by the ICBC estimator * coverage, accident details, or liability * nature or extent of unrelated damage, or * value of the vehicle after repairs | The program participant may refer the customer to ICBC for assistance. |
| * customer requests a cash settlement, or * Mitchell Connect generates a message stating the customer must contact ICBC | The program participant must refer the customer to ICBC. |
