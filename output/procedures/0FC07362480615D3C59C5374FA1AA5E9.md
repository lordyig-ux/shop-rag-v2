# Original Equipment Manufacturer glass

Description of an Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) glass.

### Description

Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) glass is a glass brand

* used by the vehicle manufacturer that displays the manufacturers’ logo, such as Ford, Chrysler, or BMW
* used originally by the vehicle manufacturer in the assembly of the motor vehicle, or
* supplied new or aftermarket, potentially.

### OEM glass suppliers

OEM brand name glass is supplied by

* the vehicle manufacturer or dealer, or
* an aftermarket supplier.
