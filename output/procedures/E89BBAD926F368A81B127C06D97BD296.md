# Tire replacement considerations

A list of criteria to review before determining whether to replace undamaged tires as part of a claim.

### OEM and tire manufacturer recommendations

When one or more tires are damaged as a result of an ICBC claim, the vehicle’s Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) or tire manufacturer may recommend the tires be replaced in sets of two or four. This ensures tires installed on the same axle meet OEM specifications and/or recommendations.

### Criteria

The following table presents considerations to review before replacing damaged tires in a claim:

| Criteria | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Tire replacement considerations | Tire replacement is dependent on  * the type of tire * the wear on the tire, and * the vehicle type. |
| Effects of tire wear and replacement | Tire wear and replacement may affect a vehicle’s on-board diagnostic equipment and safety items including  * steering * handling * braking, and * traction control. |
| Overriding consideration | When considering a claim to replace an undamaged tire, vehicle safety must be a consideration. |
