# Standards of performance for suppliers on exchange parts

Guidelines for standards of performance on exchange parts for suppliers.

### Standards of performance

Exchange parts suppliers must meet the following standards of performance and all general standards of performance.

| Requirement | Standard of performance |
| --- | --- |
| Price guarantee | Exchange parts pricing listed on the Estimate or Appraisal must be used by the listed part supplier for a period of 30 days from the date the estimate was written. |
| Readily available parts | All exchange parts listed in the Mitchell Cloud Estimating (MCE) database must be readily available within 24 hours. |
| Problem with a part | When a problem arises with the original exchange part provided, the  * supplier must locate an in stock replacement part from another part supplier in the MCE suite database, and * original exchange part supplier must make arrangements,whenever practical, to resolve the problem on the same day. |
| Exchange versus aftermarket parts | Aftermarket parts must not be provided in lieu of an exchange part. |
| Plastic bumper cores | Aftermarket plastic bumpers must not be used as Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) exchange cores. |
