# Allowable removal and installation times

Guidelines for which parts are allowed to be removed and installed to complete a refinishing task.

### Allowable removal and installation times

Sometimes parts must be removed to complete a refinishing task.

Removal and installation (RE & I) time is allowed as follows:

| When ... | Then this is allowed ... |
| --- | --- |
| an undamaged part must be removed  * for refinishing * on a blended panel | * full Mitchell Cloud Estimating (MCE) RE & I time, and * if required, additional allowance is provided for re-taping stick-on mouldings, nameplates, etc.   Note: If no RE & I time is listed in the MCE software, reasonable judgement time is allowed. |
| a visible, exposed panel requiring refinishing is shrouded by another part preventing proper paint application | RE & I only as it is necessary to provide sufficient access to the required panel. This may be  * partial (judgement) RE & I, or * full MCE RE & I. |
