# Pinstripe prices and rates

List of pinstripe prices and rates when adhesive stripes must be replaced.

### Prices and rates

When adhesive stripes must be replaced, the following price and rates apply.

| Stripe | Price | Rate (hours) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First panel | $6.00 | .2 |
| Each additional panel | Incl. | .1 |
| Maximum per side | $6.00 | .5 |
| Maximum for entire vehicle | $12.00 | 1.0 |

Note: For information regarding painted pinstripes, refer to the topic, [ICBC refinish times not in the database](4BDD31B6B74230F886B36D3AE64D28A4.html "A list of ICBC refinish times not in the Mitchell Cloud Estimating database for cars, pick-up trucks, or vans.").
