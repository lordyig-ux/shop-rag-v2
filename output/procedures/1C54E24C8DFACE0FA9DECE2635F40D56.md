# Determine whether to replace undamaged tires

Steps to determine whether to replace undamaged tires on a vehicle.

### When this applies

When a repair facility requests to replace an undamaged tire(s) on the same axle as a damaged tire being replaced.

Note: Vehicle safety must be a consideration, when considering a claim to replace an undamaged tire.

### Procedure

1. Determine whether to approve tire replacement:

   | If ... | Then ... |
   | --- | --- |
   | * the OEM and/or tire manufacturer recommends replacing undamaged tires when a tire(s) on a corresponding axle(s) is being replaced, and * the replacement tire(s) is the exact make, size, speed rating, load index and tread pattern as the remaining undamaged tire(s) | approve replacement of the undamaged tire(s). |
   | * the OEM and/or tire manufacturer does **not** recommend replacing undamaged tires when tire(s) on corresponding axles are being replaced, or * the undamaged tire(s) has 2/32" or less of wear left, or * the undamaged tire(s) has uneven tread wear due to incorrect tire pressure, misalignment or damage, or * tires have been changed previously and are already different on corresponding axles, or * one or more of the tires on the vehicle do not match the damaged tire, or * the tire has previous non-claim related damage | do not approve replacement of undamaged tire(s). |

   Note: When the OEM and tire manufacturer recommendations differ, follow the OEM recommendations.
2. Complete task.
