# Standards of performance for estimators on radiator and air conditioner parts

Guidelines for estimators on radiator and air conditioner parts.

### Standards of performance

Estimators must meet these standards of performance for radiators and air conditioners:

| Requirement | Standard of performance |
| --- | --- |
| Competitively priced replacement parts | When a radiator is determined as not being repairable, estimators must pre-price radiator and air conditioning parts according to the price available from  * the parts listed in the Mitchell database, or * Car-Part.com, using the iPro/Car-Part Pro integrated locator within Mitchell estimating.   Note: All parts that are pre-priced on the Estimate or Appraisal form must appear in the pre-priced column. |
