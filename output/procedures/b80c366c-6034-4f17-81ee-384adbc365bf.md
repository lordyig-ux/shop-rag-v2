# Managing glass claim when Glass Web Express is unavailable

Glass facilities who access the Glass Web Express (GWE) manage a glass claim differently when the GWE is unavailable.

### When this applies

When GWE is unavailable to process a glass claim for customers who have reported their damage directly to a facility.

### Procedure

1. **Glass facility**
   1. Confirms customer information is correct for

      * vehicle plate
      * vehicle information number (VIN)
      * vehicle registration number
      * date of loss (DOL)
      * cause of damage, and
      * applicable taxes (GST and PST).

      Note: When determining taxes, refer to the topic, Apply taxes to glass only claim at a glass repair program facility.
   2. Contact Provincial Glass Operations (PGO) for assistance.
2. **PGO**
   1. Confirms if

   * vehicle coverage is valid, and
   * claim qualifies for express handling.

   | When coverage and express qualification are … | Then PGO … |
   | --- | --- |
   | confirmed | * advises the facility to proceed, and * resubmits the claims request when the GWE system is restored. |
   | not confirmed | advises the facility they have to wait until the system is restored to handle the customer’s claim. |
