# Tow and storage coverage for own damage claims

A list of eligible tow and storage Own Damage claims.

### Eligible claims

ICBC pays the towing and/or storage for Own Damage (first party) claims for the following circumstances:

| Claim type | Eligibility criteria | Expenses paid by ICBC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Own Damage – personal or commercial vehicle | * Customer has ICBC Own Damage coverage for damaged vehicle, and * Claims representative has authorized the claim for one or more of the combination vehicles, or * Claim is under investigation   Note: The damage does not have to exceed the deductible. | Claim required towing and storage expenses. |
| Own Damage – combination vehicle  Note: For example – tractor and trailer. | * Customer has ICBC Own Damage coverage for damaged vehicle, and the attached vehicle(s) is undamaged, and * ICBC has authorized the claim . * Claim is under investigation | * Claim Required towing and storage expenses for damaged vehicle, and * Claim required first tow expenses for the undamaged attached vehicle(s) |
