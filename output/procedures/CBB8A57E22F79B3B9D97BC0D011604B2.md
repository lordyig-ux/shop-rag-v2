# Headlamp adjustments on heavy equipment

Summary of headlamp adjustments on heavy equipment.

### Allowance

A stand-alone labour time applies for adjusting the headlamps on heavy equipment estimates.

Heavy equipment headlamp adjustments are allowed 0.5 hours.
