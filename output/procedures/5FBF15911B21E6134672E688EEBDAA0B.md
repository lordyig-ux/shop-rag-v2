# Freight charges on Original Equipment Manufacturer parts

Policy on freight charges on Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) parts.

### General freight charges

Reasonable freight charges incurred due to no OEM dealer in the local area may become a part of the overall repair costs.

To be allowable as part of overall repair costs freight charges must:

* not be marked-up
* be substantiated by original invoices and
* be listed in the applicable parts column of the Estimate or Appraisal form, as they are subject to taxes.

Air freight or special delivery charges are considered only when:

* loss of use exposure is a factor, and
* the claims office has pre-authorized such charges.
