# Repair facility requirements for recycled parts

Requirements for a repair facility to use recycled parts on an ICBC’s customer vehicle.

### Requirements

The table presents the requirements that a repair facility must have when using recycled parts:

| Requirement | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Parts check | * All recycled parts must be visually inspected for any shipping damage by the repair facility prior to signing the delivery driver’s invoice. * If the repair facility cannot inspect the part upon delivery, they have one (1) business day to:   + inspect the part for any damages, and   + return the part for a full refund or exchange. |
| Usability and safety parts | Whenever it is practical, it is the responsibility of the repair facility to:  * determine the usability of the recycled parts, and * ensure there is no compromise of the safety of the vehicle prior to installation within three (3) business days of receiving the part. |
| Return policy | If a recycled part is not acceptable, the repair facility will contact the recycled part supplier directly to return the part. All returned parts must be returned:   * in the same condition as when received * packaged appropriately to prevent damage, and * returned within 30 days of the initial sale whenever possible. |
| Administration and ordering | The repair facility:  * verifies with ICBC that the vehicle repairs can proceed before the repair facility can order the parts * must order all parts by ICBC claim number, and * attach a copy of the original invoice indicating the ICBC claim number to every repair account submitted for payment. |
| Part and supplier standards and warranty | Repair facility is responsible to ensure the recycled part supplier and the parts used meets the requirements of the topic, [Recycled parts requirements](FC0C1F21820105355AB95DA3F96E6725.html "Requirements for a recycled part to be used on an ICBC’s customer vehicle."). Note: When the recycled part supplier does not meet all the requirements, the repair facility must assume all warranty for the part. |
| Define required cuts | Repair facility must clearly define the requirements of all welded panel cuts after it has been verified that the vehicle can be repaired. |
| Labour changes | All labour changes to the Estimate or Appraisal must be approved by an ICBC estimator. |
| Labour compensation | Repair facility requests for labour compensation on damaged parts supplied must be reasonable, and agreed to by the recycler before the parts are repaired or installed. |
