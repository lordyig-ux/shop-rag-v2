# Refinish allowance

Requirements for refinish allowances.

### Requirements

Refinish times for most refinish items are listed in Mitchell Cloud Estimating (MCE).

Refinish allowances are subject to the following requirements:

* Refinish time for items listed in MCE must be applied as listed, and
* Items requiring refinishing that are not listed in MCE, are assigned judgment times.

### Coverage

In the event of a claim, ICBC

* pays the cost of repainting the damaged portion of the vehicle only, and
* does not make an allowance for complete painting of the vehicle to resolve a problem with colour match.
