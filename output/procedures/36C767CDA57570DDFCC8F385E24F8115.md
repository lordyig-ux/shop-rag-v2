# Glass material allowances

List of glass material allowances.

### Urethane and butyl tape allowances

Vehicles that require a urethane or butyl tape set according to National Autoglass Specifications (NAGS) are eligible for allowances.

The table presents the urethane and butyl tape allowances:

| Item | Allowance |
| --- | --- |
| Urethane set glass installation allowance, per glass part | $48.00 |
| Butyl tape allowance per glass part | $12.50 |

### Urethane allowance

The urethane allowance includes

* conventional urethane
* two-component rapid cure urethane, and
* high modulus/non-conductive urethane.

Note: The glass facility is responsible to ensure the correct urethane is used at all times.

### Allowances apply

Allowances apply as follows:

| Type of allowance | Allowance applies |
| --- | --- |
| Urethane | The urethane allowance   * applies for all windshield, rear glass and quarter glass claims when indicated as urethane installation in National Autoglass Specifications (NAGS), and * does not apply for door glass installation except for permanently urethane-fixed (non-movable) glass in van and cargo doors.   Important: Up to three urethane allowances can be added per claim installation for Class A motorhomes. |
| Butyl tape | The butyl tape allowance applies for stationary back and/or 1/4 glass, requiring the use of butyl tape as identified in the NAGS Calculator. |
