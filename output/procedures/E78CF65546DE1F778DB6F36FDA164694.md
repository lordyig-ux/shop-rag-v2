# Alternative Transportation Service

Alternative Transportation Service (ATS) is a mandatory requirement of the collision repair program.

ATS is a service offered by the collision repair program participant to customers, which

* applies to all claims regardless of the cause of loss, subject to ATS exceptions
* covers the period from the ATS responsibility date until vehicle repairs are complete, and
* satisfies the alternative transportation requirements of customers with an authorized estimate.

For further information, refer to section 9, Alternative Transportation Service and Rental, of the [Collision Repair program guide](https://www.icbc.com/partners/material-damage/Documents/collision-program-guide.pdf).
