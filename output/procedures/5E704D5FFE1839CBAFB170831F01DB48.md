# Glass moulding and clips allowances

List of glass moulding and clips allowances for windshields and back glass.

### Allowances

Glass moulding and clips are assigned allowances.

| Item | Allowance |
| --- | --- |
| Moulding | $45.26 |
| Standard clips | $0.00 |
| Specialty clips | Retail price, or in the absence of a retail price, cost plus 25%. |

### Moulding allowance

The moulding allowance is only payable when the new moulding(s) have a combined total price below $101.00 per piece of glass. The allowance can be applied to each piece of glass on a claim, if applicable.

### Clips allowance

ICBC does not pay for standard clips as they are considered shop supplies. Invoices for specialty clips are paid only when Provincial Glass Operations (PGO) has authorized payment in advance, where the invoice shows the

* quantity of clips used, and
* list and retail prices.

Note: If no retail price is shown on the part invoice then the facility may bill cost plus 25%.
