# Initiating glass claim on trade-in vehicles policy

Customers must initiate a glass claim prior to trading in their vehicle at a dealership.

### Policy

When a customer is trading in their vehicle at a dealership, they must initiate a glass claim prior to transferring title to the dealership.

Important: The repairs must be completed within seven (7) days of the trade-in to the dealership, including all calibrations covered by the claim. Exceptions must be pre-authorized by Provincial Glass Operations (PGO) and be noted on the claim.

### Initiate claim

The customer can initiate the glass claim in the following ways.

| Customer access | Who the customer must contact | Repair shop action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer has access to a glass repair program facility | The glass repair program facility | * ensure that the registered owner signs the work order, and * collect deductible. |
| Customer lives in a remote area and does not have access to a glass repair program facility | The Claims Contact Centre (CCC) |
