# Entegral

Entegral is an integrated software platform that enables communication and collaboration between ICBC and collision repair and glass repair program participants.

Entegral gives ICBC staff the capability to manage the application and enrollment of prospective applicants to both the collision and glass repair programs, as well as monitoring and managing program participants through audits and analytics.
