# Standards of performance for alternative parts suppliers on aftermarket parts

Guidelines for standards of performance on alternative parts supplier on aftermarket parts.

### Standards of performance

Aftermarket parts suppliers must meet the following standards of performance, and all general standards of performance:

| Requirement | Standard of performance |
| --- | --- |
| Price guarantee | Aftermarket parts pricing listed on the Estimate or Appraisal form must be used by the listed part supplier for a period of 30 days from the date the estimate was written. |
| Parts availability | All aftermarket parts listed in the Mitchell Cloud Estimating (MCE) database must be readily available within 24 hours. |

Note: Parts pricing on the estimate may be below retail pricing but not below parts suppliers or participants cost.
