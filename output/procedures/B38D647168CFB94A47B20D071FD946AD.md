# Supplier trade qualification policy

Policy on trade qualification for ICBC’s suppliers’ technical positions who perform repairs on customer vehicles.

### Policy

The policy applies to the following technical positions:

* auto body collision technician
* automotive refinishing technician
* automotive refinishing prep technician
* automotive service technician
* automotive glass technician
* heavy duty equipment technician
* recreational vehicle service technician
* transport trailer technician
* motorcycle technician, and
* truck and transport mechanic.

The policy ensures that all repairs:

* are performed by qualified technicians
* meets a professional standard, and
* are in accordance to ICBC’s best practices and requirements.

Note: Trade equivalency (Supervision and Sign Off Authority) is not accepted as a trade qualification. It is strictly allowed only for the supervision of an apprentice, and does not provide certification to perform repairs on ICBC customer vehicles.

### Eligibility

The individual must provide a copy of their driver’s licence or photo identification to verify their employment, and must:

* have a valid and recognized trade qualification acquired within BC or another Canadian province, or
* be registered into an apprenticeship agreement for the specific trade they perform within the supplier facility.

  Note: Individuals, including either a certified journeyperson or someone with equivalent sign off authority, must work for the same employer that the apprentice is sponsored by, but do not have to work on-site at the same location on a full-time basis.
