# Requirements for aluminum material sublet charge on Audi vehicles

Requirements for an aluminum material sublet charge on Audi vehicles.

### Requirements

An aluminum material sublet charge can be paid on the Mitchell Cloud Estimating (MCE)Estimate or Appraisal for Audi vehicles, when the following requirements are met:

* vehicle is an Audi model with damage to the aluminum components requiring Audi approved materials, or
* damages affect the structural integrity of a body seam that requires Audi approved materials to be used to repair the vehicle
* repair facility is an Audi approved facility for aluminum repairs, and
* Audi approved materials are purchased through an Audi dealership, and used for the repairs.

Note: There is only one allowance per qualified claim.
