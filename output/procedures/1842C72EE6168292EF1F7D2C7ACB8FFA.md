# Storing propane and natural gas fuelled vehicles policy

Policy on vehicles fuelled by liquid propane gas (LPG) or compressed natural gas (CNG) stored in ICBC facility.

### Eligibility

The policy applies to any vehicle fuelled by LPG or CNG, whether damaged or not.

### When it applies

The policy applies to storage

* overnight
* on weekends, or
* on holidays.

### Policy

Vehicles fuelled by liquid propane gas (LPG) or compressed natural gas (CNG) must not be stored inside ICBC buildings on corporate property

* overnight
* on weekends, or
* during holidays.

Note: The Estimating Services Manager is responsible for enforcing this policy.

### Rationale

This policy is in effect to limit fire and explosion hazards.
