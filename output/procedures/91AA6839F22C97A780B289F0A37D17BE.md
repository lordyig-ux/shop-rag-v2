# Allowance

Definition of an allowance.

### Definition

An allowance is a reduction to the amount ICBC would normally pay to repair, replace or paint the part(s), due to pre-existing or unrelated damage on the vehicle.

### Allowance requirements

The estimator must

* discuss the allowance with the vehicle owner or their representative, and
* list it on the Estimate or Appraisal.

### Allowance and collision repair program participants

A collision repair program participant that assesses the vehicle and prepares the estimate must

* ensure that the vehicle damage being estimated was incurred as a result of the accident being claimed
* identify all pre-existing or unrelated damage, and
* document all pre-existing or unrelated damage in the "Remarks" section of the Estimate or Appraisal.
