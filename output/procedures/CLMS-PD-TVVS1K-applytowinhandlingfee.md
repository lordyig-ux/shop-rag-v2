# Apply the tow-in handling fee to an estimate

Collision repair program participants can submit an estimate including the tow-in handling fee in certain circumstances.

### When this applies

Participants are eligible to charge ICBC the tow-in handling fee, listed on the [Collision Repair Facility Labour Rates and Material Allowances](https://www.icbc.com/partners/material-damage/Documents/collision-facility-labour-rates-material-allowances.pdf), if the

* vehicle is towed in and accepted by the participant, and
* estimate is completed and submitted to ICBC for review.

  Note: Obvious total losses should be refused by the participant unless requested by ICBC. The tow-in handling fee does not apply to obvious total losses.

Important: If a vehicle driven to the collision repair program facility is later deemed a non-drive by the participant, it is not eligible for the tow-in handling fee.

### Procedure

1. Select the TOW-IN HANDLING fee from the My Templates section of Mitchell Cloud Estimating (MCE) and add it to the initial estimate.
2. Complete and submit estimate.

   Note: The tow-in handling fee can be charged in addition to the total loss handling fee or repair planning and documentation fee, when applicable.
