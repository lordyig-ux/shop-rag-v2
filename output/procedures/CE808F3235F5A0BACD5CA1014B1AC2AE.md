# Pinchweld refinish allowance

Guidelines to apply pinchweld refinish allowances.

### Refinish allowance

Refinish allowances apply to pinchwelds as follows when:

* pinchwelds are unavoidably damaged by sill clamps, an allowance per side for spot refinishing applies, and
* there is a need to weld braces or drill holes, judgement time is allowed to refinish these areas.
