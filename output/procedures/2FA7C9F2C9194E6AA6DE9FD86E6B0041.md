# Take digital images

Steps on taking digital images for a claim.

### When this applies

Estimators take, load and save required images of the vehicle in WorkCenter (WC) according to distinct specifications. Images are a key requirement when documenting a claim.

### Procedure

1. Take the required images according to image specifications.
2. Save images to WC.
