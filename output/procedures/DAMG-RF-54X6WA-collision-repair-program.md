# Collision repair program

ICBC and Collision Repair Program participants work cooperatively to establish an effective and efficient business relationship for the benefit of their mutual customers.

### Program overview

The Collision Repair Program

* accredits facilities that achieve and maintain the highest standards for customer service and quality repairs
* ensures that customers receive efficient, cost-effective repairs that meet the highest industry standards for safe, proper repairs
* provides participants with access to business systems and software to estimate repairs to ICBC customer vehicles, and
* recognizes the investments that participants make in training, equipment, productivity and customer service.

Participation in the program is voluntary for collision repair facilities that

* meet and maintain the program requirements, and
* sign the Collision Repair Agreement.

Participants must adhere to requirements set out in the

* Damage section of Claims Procedures
* Collision Repair Program guide, and
* Collision Repair Agreement.

### Benefits

The benefits of the program include

* increased facility efficiency
* reduced repair cycle times for customers, from date of the accident to completed repair
* ICBC and participants working together to continuously improve service efficiency to customers
* eligibility to receive maximum labour rates set out on the ICBC’s Business Partner’s Page (as amended from time to time)
* enhanced information for customers regarding Participants’ repair capabilities, such as
  + language options
  + original equipment manufacturer (OEM) certifications
  + aluminium repair, and
  + other customer relevant information
