# Commercial mechanical rates

Guidelines for commercial mechanical rates to be applied to the Estimate or Appraisal.

### Rates

The mechanical rates apply when repairing the:

* engine
* transmission
* clutch
* flywheel, and
* differential (gear work) of commercial vehicles.

Note: The excluded items listed above are paid by following the body labour and material rates.

### Parts

Appraisers indicate in the repair estimate which part applies to the mechanical rate by:

* completing a separate suffix Estimate or Appraisal that describes the part covered by the mechanical rate, or
* circling the flat rate labour entry for the operation on the original Estimate or Appraisal.

Note: The mechanical repair times used on the repair estimate are in the Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) warranty flat rate manual.
