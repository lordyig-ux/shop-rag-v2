# Authorize an initial rental vehicle request during first notice of loss

How to review the initial rental vehicle request before submitting a rental vehicle authorization.

### Prerequisites

The customer's vehicle is non-driveable.

### When this applies

The customer requests a rental vehicle during first notice of loss (FNOL).

Note: Before a rental vehicle is authorized, the customer must select a rental vehicle provider. ICBC recommends that customers use one of its preferred vendors; however, it is the customer's decision on which rental vehicle provider to use.

### Procedure

1. Review and confirm the following claim details, including

   * the customer's
     + available coverage which could include
       - RoadStar
       - Roadside Plus
       - Optional Loss of Use, or
       - Loss of Use coverage under Basic Vehicle Damage coverage
     + daily and maximum coverage limits, if applicable
     + policy expiry dates
     + vehicle make and model, and
     + goods and services tax (GST) registrant status information is correct
   * responsibility, and
   * regional location.

   Note: For Enterprise Rent-A-Car rental vehicles, the "Other" postal code field is used when the customer will pick up the rental vehicle from a location other than the Enterprise branch closest to their home address. For more details, refer to the ClaimCenter User Guider procedure, [Postal codes for cities where Enterprise Rent-A-Car is the preferred vendor](Topic.aspx?rootmap=ClaimCenter-user-guide&topic=CCUG-RF-A7Z3AM-00-postal-codes-enterprise-is-preferred "A list of postal codes that can be used when “Other” is selected in the Rental screen for an rental vehicle authorization.").
2. Determine if the initial rental vehicle request can be authorized.

   Note: If there is a concern about the claim, such as a breach, advise the customer that payment of their rental vehicle fees receive consideration on a reimbursement basis.

   | If the initial rental request is ... | Then ... |
   | --- | --- |
   | authorized for a rental vehicle from Enterprise Rent-A-Car | * create and submit the rental vehicle authorization though the Rental screen in the New Claim Wizard, and * advise the customer that Enterprise Rent-A-Car will contact them within one hour to confirm their rental vehicle needs. Note: For further information on how to create and submit an authorization for Enterprise Rent-A-Car, refer to the procedure, [Create a rental authorization with Enterprise Rent-A-Car](Topic.aspx?rootmap=ClaimCenter-user-guide&topic=CCUG-PD-3TB2W3-00-create-a-rental-authorization-with-enterprise-rent-a-car "How to create and submit a rental vehicle authorization to preferred supplier, .").  Important: If the rental vehicle is not required immediately, advise the customer to contact their claims representative to arrange for a rental vehicle for the dates they require it. |
   | authorized for a rental vehicle from a rental vehicle provider other than Enterprise Rent-A-Car | advise the customer to contact the rental vehicle provider directly to arrange for a rental vehicle. Note: If the rental vehicle is for loss of use on an out of province claim, advise the customer that ICBC processes these costs on a reimbursement basis. |
   | not authorized | advise the customer why they do not have authorization for a rental vehicle. |
3. Save and assign the claim.
4. Create the exposure and reserve line for the applicable loss of use coverage, if required.
