# Take an enquiry for child car seat reimbursement or replacement

Steps to take an enquiry for reimbursement and/or replacement of a child car seat, and to advise the customer how to apply to ICBC.

### Prerequisites

A customer has reported a claim that had a child car seat damaged.

### When this applies

A customer is enquiring about reimbursement and/or replacement of a child car seat.

Note: If a customer is enquiring about child car seat passenger safety, redirect the customer to contact the British Columbia Automobile Association (BCAA) Child Passenger Safety Program Information Hotline by phone at 1-877-247-5551.

### Procedure

1. Advise the customer that the details for ICBC's a child car seat reimbursement request process are available online at [Get reimbursed for your car seat or booster seat (icbc.com)](https://www.icbc.com/claims/repair-replace/car-seat-reimbursement). 

   | If the customer ... | Then ... |
   | --- | --- |
   | requires additional guidance | advise the customer that by completing the reimbursement request through the online claims service (Claims Portal) or the PDF request form available on icbc.com they will either * receive a reimbursement for either the damaged child car seat or replacement child car seat with a receipt, or * receive a reimbursement of a pre-set amount based on declaration of child car seat type. Note: The PDF request form should only be used if a customer is unable to use the Claims Portal. |
   | does not require additional guidance | * end the call with the customer, and * proceed to step 4. |
2. Provide additional details to the customer, using the procedure, [Child car seat reimbursement types and eligibility policy](Policy-on-car-seat-reimbursement-criteria.html "ICBC provides reimbursement for damaged or replaced , or a set amount.").
3. Provide the customer with details to set up direct deposit to receive reimbursement by electronic funds transfer (EFT).

   Note: Refer to [Set up direct deposit to receive your payments](https://www.icbc.com/claims/report-view/Pages/set-up-direct-deposit.aspx) on icbc.com for EFT information.
4. Create a note to record a summary of the details exchanged during the call.
