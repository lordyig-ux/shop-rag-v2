# Tire replacement policy

Policy on replacing tires for the Estimate or Appraisal form.

### Policy

The following apply to all tire replacements:

* All tires must be pre-priced by the estimator on the original Estimate or Appraisal. The prices listed must reflect a competitive ICBC price.
* Depreciation applies to all replacement tires.
* When a replacement tire is no longer available and there are no comparable matches available for a new tire, ICBC’s obsolete parts policy applies.

Note: The liability of the Corporation for loss or damage to an obsolete part of an insured vehicle is limited to the price at which the part was last listed on the manufacturer’s price list.
