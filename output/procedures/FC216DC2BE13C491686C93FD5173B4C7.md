# Heavy equipment front and rear axle wheel alignment allowances

Summary of the wheel alignment allowance for commercial and heavy equipment wheel alignments completed in house by a repair facility.

### Allowance entitlements

Wheel alignments completed in-house at a repair facility, have an established labour time.

The flat rate allowance applicable when performing tractor and trailer alignments in house at the repair facility.

| When the wheel alignment is ... | Then the facility is entitled to a flat rate of... |
| --- | --- |
| to the front axle, including checking alignment and setting toe-in | 3.0 hours.  Note: Labour time does not include adjustment of rear axles. |
| adjusting the front axle, including set back, if required | 1.0 hours. |
| a three axle alignment | 5.0 hours, including adjustments. |
| a four axle alignment | 6.0 hours, including adjustments. |

Important: In circumstances where alignments are not completed in-house by the repair facility and are instead sublet to another repair facility, the repair facility subletting the alignments is entitled to a mark-up of 25% over cost.
