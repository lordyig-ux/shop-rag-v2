# Manage an Ignition Interlock device

How an estimator or Collision Repair Program Participant handles a vehicle where an ignition interlock device is present.

### Prerequisites

An ignition interlock device was involved in a crash.

### When this applies

The claim owner or exposure owner has reviewed the claim and determined there may be coverage for the repair of the ignition interlock device.

### Procedure

1. Receive the appropriate EST activity for the non-drive vehicle.
2. Determine how the ignition interlock device will be handled.

   | If the ignition interlock device handset or interface module is ... | Then ... |
   | --- | --- |
   | undamaged | document any vehicle damage on the Claim Estimate (CL14) only. |
   | damaged as a result of the crash, and the customer is not responsible | enter instructions on the Claim Estimate (CL14) to contact Smart Start Canada to arrange for the repair or replacement of the interface and headset. |
   | damaged as a result of the crash, and the customer is responsible | * direct the customer to deal directly with Smart Start Canada regarding repairs and repair costs, and * provide the Smart Canada phone number 1-844-432-4776. |

   Note: When Smart Start Canada charges for their work, the repair facility shows the invoice as a "sublet" on the Claim Estimate (CL14).
