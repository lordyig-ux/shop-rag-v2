# Replacement policy on automotive audio-visual electronic equipment

Summary of Replacement policy for automotive audio-visual electronic equipment.

### Classification of equipment

All these items are considered sound and communication equipment:

* global positioning systems (GPS)
* monitors
* headsets, and
* video and DVD players.

### Requirements for coverage

Most sound and communication equipment items are subject to similar requirements to be covered, they must be

* permanently attached to a permanently attached part of the vehicle, or
* installed by means of a shuttle mount.

### Coverage

| When the sound and communication equipment ... | Then it is covered... |
| --- | --- |
| is supplied by or available from the manufacturer of the vehicle | as OEM equipment. |
| is not supplied by or available from the manufacturer of the vehicle | under these sections of Division 5 of the ICBC Autoplan Optional Policy   * Section 5.11 (b) (iv) * Section 5.11 (c)(iii), and * Section 5.12 (v). |
