# Effect of special endorsements on collision repair program facilities

Summary of impacts of special endorsements on collision repair program facilities

### Collision repair program participants responsibility

Collision repair program participants are responsible for confirming whether either of the following coverages are in effect

* Replacement Cost Endorsement (APV286A), or
* New Vehicle Replacement Plus (NVR+).

### Effects of coverage

The following apply to claims with either a APV286A or NVR+ in effect:

* Mitchell Connect identifies these collision repair program estimates or supplements to both the program participant and estimator via assignment details
* only new Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) parts are to be utilized, and
* no depreciation applies to the parts or labour.
