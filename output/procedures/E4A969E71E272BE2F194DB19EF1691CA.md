# Estimate explanation requirement

List of items that the estimator must explain to the customer on the vehicle estimate and/or repairs.

### Items to explain

The estimator must explain the estimate to the vehicle owner, including the following:

* any repair-related procedures
* the items listed on the estimates
* requirements to pay any deductibles and/or depreciation
* requirements to pay any applicable co-insurance amount, and
* any clarification of situations involving pre-existing or unrelated damage.

Note: This also applies to claims handled by collision repair program participants that are referred back to ICBC.
