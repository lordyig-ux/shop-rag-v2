# Glass claim reporting

Customers can report a glass claim directly to a glass repair program facility.

### Glass claim reporting details

Glass repair program participants must obtain authorization from Provincial Glass Operations (PGO) before starting repairs on a vehicle that has additional non-glass damage.

Note: Glass repair claims may include run-channels, mouldings, and trim. These items do not count as additional non-glass damage, and therefore do not require PGO authorization.

A claim is not eligible for glass repair under the Glass Repair Program when the

* incident occurred outside of British Columbia (B.C.), or
* damage is due to a collision.

Glass claims are managed or created by the Customer Contact Centre (CCC) if

* the customer reporting the claim does not have access to a glass repair facility, or
* there is damage to the
  + headlights
  + tail lights
  + sun roof
  + mirrors
  + bodywork, or
  + paint.

CCC does not retain the claim if it is a subrogated claim, or if it has a coverage issue.
