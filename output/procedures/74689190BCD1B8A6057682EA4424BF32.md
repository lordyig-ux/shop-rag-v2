# Process claim for split windshield

How to process a split windshield claim when there are two glass panes that are damaged at the same time.

### When this applies

When a customer has a claim for a split windshield, the claim owner must assist in identifying whether the damage resulted from

* one incident, or
* multiple incidents.

This is to determine whether the customer has one claim with one deductible to be paid, or two claims with a deductible paid on each claim.

### Procedure

1. Determine whether the two glass panes were damaged at the same time.

   Note:
   * Sometimes the claim owner may consult with the estimator on the damage to assist in determining the number of claims.
   * There are two claims and two deductibles paid when the customer advises that both panes of glass were damaged at different times, or the investigation determines this same conclusion.

   | If both glass panes were damaged at ... | Then ... |
   | --- | --- |
   | the same time | * process the two panes of the split windshield as one claim * send an ICBC Glass Claim Information (CL462C) form to the customer, and * create a note explaining the reason for one claim. |
   | different times | * process the two panes of the split windshield as two separate claims * send two CL462C forms to the customer * create a note documenting the reason for two claims, and * create a "Prior claims" association between the two claims . |
2. Advise the customer the outcome.
3. Ensure any documentation the customer provided is attached to the claim.
