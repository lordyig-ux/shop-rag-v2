# Vehicle rental rates policy

ICBC will authorize only preferred rates except where the exposure owner has deemed a different rate as reasonable under the circumstances.

### Policy

Unless there is a note with the Topic of "Rental Vehicle" on the claim, the claims admin (LOU team) can only authorize the rate for a similar-sized vehicle, as outlined in the ICBC rental rate structure and as offered by preferred rental vehicle providers.

### Loss of Use coverage

In LOU scenarios, ICBC can specify the make and model of available substitute vehicle when providing alternate transportation to a customer. The expense incurred for the alternate transportation cannot exceed either the per-day limit or the total limit of the coverage purchased by the insured and described in the insured's owner's certificate.

If the customer wants to rent a more expensive vehicle, the LOU representative must confirm with the exposure owner. Otherwise, the customer must pay the difference in price.

### Third Party Liability

For customers who do not have LOU coverage but who have a third party liability claim against another ICBC insured, ICBC may cover the cost of renting a similar-sized substitute vehicle up to a daily and total limit deemed reasonable by the exposure owner.
