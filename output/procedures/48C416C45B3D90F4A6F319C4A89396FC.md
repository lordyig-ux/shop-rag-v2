# Original Equipment Manufacturer surplus parts requirements

Requirements for Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) surplus parts.

### Parts requirements and standards

OEM surplus parts must meet all the following standards to be on the Estimate or Appraisal:

| Requirement | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Freight | All OEM surplus parts' prices contained in the Mitchell Cloud Estimating (MCE) database must include freight to any location within the province of British Columbia. |
| Availability | All OEM surplus parts contained in the MCE database must be readily available within two working days. |

Note: Parts pricing on the estimate may be below retail pricing but not below parts suppliers or participants cost.
