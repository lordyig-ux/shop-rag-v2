# Requirements for sublet towing service billing

List of requirements for sublet towing services invoices.

### Requirements

When a sublet towing company has been used, the original tow company must attach a copy of the company’s invoice to the Towing Invoice (CL14C) form that they submit to ICBC.

A sublet towing company’s invoice must include the following:

* company name
* number of employees
* retail charge out rate
* trade discount, if any
* length of time at the motor vehicle crash location
* arrival and departure times
* travel time
* distance travelled
* signature
* date, and
* explanations or photos documenting reasons for the sublet charges.
