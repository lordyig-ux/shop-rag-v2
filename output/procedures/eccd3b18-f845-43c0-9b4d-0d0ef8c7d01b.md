# Environmental handling charges

List of environmental handling charges that ICBC suppliers pay when they purchase specified products to complete the customer’s vehicle repairs.

### Charges

ICBC suppliers pay the environmental handling charges on Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) supplied equipment.

ICBC reimburses their suppliers on these environmental handling charges when the charges are applied to the customer’s vehicle repairs.

Environmental handling charges for purchased products are in the following table:

| Specified products | Environmental charges |
| --- | --- |
| Oil | $0.05 per litre |
| Oil containers | $0.10 per litre of contents |
| Anti-freeze | $0.20 per litre |
| Anti-freeze containers | $0.10 per litre of contents |
| Oil filters | $0.55 equal to or smaller than 8 inches  $1.25 greater than 8 inches |
