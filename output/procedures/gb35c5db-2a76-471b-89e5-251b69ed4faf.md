# Compensation for labour on recycled parts

Summary of recycled parts that are compensated for labour.

When a recycled parts supplier provides a part that requires repairing or refurbishing, then compensation for labour must be

* fair and mutually agreeable by repair facility and the recycled parts supplier, and
* paid by the recycled part supplier before the part is installed.

### Exceptions

ICBC may approve compensation for labour when:

* recycled part is returned for reasons beyond the recycler’s control, and
* custom cutting of sheet metal recycled parts on a vehicle reduces or eliminates the opportunity for resale.

### Example

Estimator determines the vehicle is repairable, but while it is being repaired it is discovered there is additional hidden damage. The additional hidden damage may cause:

* repairs dollar amount to increase, and
* vehicle becomes a total loss.
