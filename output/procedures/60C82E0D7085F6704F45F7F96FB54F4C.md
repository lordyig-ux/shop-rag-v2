# Replace propane or natural gas decal

Steps on replacing propane or natural gas decals for new windshield.

### When this applies

When a windshield with a propane or natural gas decal needs replacing the customer must replace that decal with one for the new windshield.

### Procedure

1. Glass facility
   1. Identify the propane or natural gas decal when verifying damage to the windshield.
2. Glass facility 
   1. Reimburses $10.00 to the customer.
   2. Provide the old decal for verification to the customer.
3. Customer
   1. Attends the appropriate government agency for replacement of the propane decal.
4. Government agency
   1. Sells the propane diamond decal or natural gas decal to the customer with appropriate proof.
