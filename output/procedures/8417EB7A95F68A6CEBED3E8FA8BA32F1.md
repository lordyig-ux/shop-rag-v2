# Collision repair program services

The collision repair program includes value-added services that a collision repair program participant offers to customers to ease the vehicle repair process.

### Collision repair program

Collision repair program participants offer convenient services that make it easier for customers to have their vehicles repaired.

### Services

Participants offer several value-added services.

| Service | Description |
| --- | --- |
| On-site damage estimates | The facility has  * access to ICBC business systems, and * software to estimate ICBC customers’ vehicles.  Note: The estimate and repairs are completed at one location for qualifying claims. |
| Provide alternative transportation | The participant provides Alternative Transportation Service (ATS) to satisfy the alternative transportation needs of customers.  Alternative transportation may include  * paying public transit costs, or * providing   + a taxi to and from work and home   + a shuttle to and from work and home   + a replacement vehicle, or   + some other form of transportation satisfactory to the customer. |
| Rental car arrangements | The participant contacts ICBC to make necessary arrangements for customers who have ICBC Loss of Use (LOU) coverage, if the customer’s transportation needs are not satisfied by ATS. |
