# Structural aluminum repair rate guidelines

Guidelines for when the structural aluminum repair rate is to be applied to the Estimate or Appraisal.

### Rate guidelines

The situations for when the structural aluminum repair rate is applicable.

| Situation | Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| When the Estimate or Appraisal includes  * repair or replacement of welded on, or * rivet bonded aluminum structural components that requires the use of   + aluminum specific equipment, and/or   + tools to install.  Note: The replacement of a welded on or rivet bonded aluminum quarter panels or bedside panels would qualify for the ICBC aluminum rate. | The Aluminum Repair rate is  * paid, and * selected in the labour type drop down menu within [Mitchell Cloud Estimating](FORM-GL-U8ND44-mitchell-cloud-estimating.html) when adding the applicable estimate line per:   + structural aluminum part repair, or   + replacement operation. |
| When the Estimate or Appraisal includes  * repair or replacement of bolt on structural aluminum components * non-structural repairs to any outer sheet aluminum panels, and/or * "PULL AND ALIGN UNIBODY" pre-store line remark | The Aluminum Repair rate is  * not paid, and * the applicable rate for the specific labour type applies to the Estimate or Appraisal.  Note: Examples are hoods, fenders, doors, and repairs to quarter panels and bedside panels, etc. The "PULL AND ALIGN UNIBODY" pre-store remark is defaulted to ICBC frame rates. |

Important: Collision repair program participants must not change labour or rate codes associated with any labour operations in [Mitchell Cloud Estimating](FORM-GL-U8ND44-mitchell-cloud-estimating.html) other than qualifying aluminum structural repair and replacements. Refer to, [Incorrect and manual labour codes in estimating system](8A7FBF641749CDE1E079059E25DAD608.html "Steps for an estimator to enter labour operations in Mitchell Cloud Estimating.") policy.
