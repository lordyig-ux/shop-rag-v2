# U-bilt vehicle total loss settlement guidelines

The total loss settlement on a u-bilt vehicle, also known as a homemade vehicle, is based on its actual cash value (ACV) including its covered equipment.

### Evaluation resources

U-bilt vehicles are often customized and unique in nature. This presents a significant challenge in developing an accurate ACV.

The ACV can be determined using the

* Vehicle Valuation report Specialty Services department
* any printed ads
* internet sites, or
* an independent appraiser (with manager approval).

### No lien search

A lien search is not required because a u-bilt vehicle does not have a vehicle identification number (VIN).

### Insurance policy

The Claims Representative must be aware of how the vehicle is insured:

* plate policy
* declared value, or
* a liable third party claim.
