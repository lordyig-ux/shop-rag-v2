# Invoicing structure of the preferred supplier agreement between ICBC and Enterprise Rent-A-Car

Under direct rental invoicing, vehicle availability is guaranteed with no-charge vehicle size upgrades, from Economy up to Standard SUV class, when the appropriate vehicle size is unavailable.

Exception: All Large SUV vehicle classes and above will require 24 hours' notice to guarantee availability.

Enterprise Rent-A-Car will

* accept vehicle returns any time within a calendar day with no additional charges
* ensure the majority of all Enterprise Rent-A-Car locations have after-hours key drop boxes
* guarantee a half tank of fuel for all rentals
* permit renters to travel to the USA with the rental vehicle, and
* provide
  + a pick-up and drop-off service to the renter at no charge within 50 kilometres of an Enterprise Rent-A-Car location
  + toll-free roadside assistance
  + unlimited kilometres for all categories, and
  + hand control vehicles upon request with 48 hours' notice.

Additional fees include

* $0.50 per kilometre pick-up and delivery fee when outside of a 50-kilometre radius from an Enterprise Rent-A-Car location, and
* $12 per day for winter tire authorization may be required at available locations and vehicle classes when auto approval does not apply.

  Note: Winter tires is an auto approved charge for Enterprise Rent-A-Car in [Northern B.C. rental rates](CLMS-RF-KQY629-rental-northern-bc.html "ICBC replacement vehicle daily rental rates for Vancouver Island (excluding Victoria) locations (Enterprise Rent-A-Car) including Dawson Creek, Fort St. John, Kitimat, Prince George, Prince Rupert, Smithers, and Terrace."), [Select Southern Interior rental rates](CLMS-RF-TK9872-rental-thompson-okanagan.html "ICBC replacement vehicle daily rental rates for select Southern Interior locations (Enterprise Rent-A-Car) including Cranbrook, Kamloops, Kelowna, and Vernon."), and [Central Interior and Cariboo rental rates](CLMS-RF-Y998MC-rental-central-interior-cariboo.html "ICBC replacement vehicle daily rental rates for Central Interior and Cariboo locations (Enterprise Rent-A-Car), including Salmon Arm, Quesnel, and Williams Lake.") between October 1 and April 30.
