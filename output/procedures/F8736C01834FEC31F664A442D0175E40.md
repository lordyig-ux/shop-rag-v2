# Panel bonding and welding policy

Policy on replacing parts and panels for panel bonding and welding.

### Policy

Replacement of parts or panels must use approved and/or recommended Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) materials and procedures.

| When a part or panel is replaced … | Then ICBC … |
| --- | --- |
| * using an approved structural adhesive * the procedure is an approved and/or recommended OEM procedure, and * using OEM approved welding | will pay the Estimate or Appraisal. |
| using a structural adhesive or procedure that is not OEM approved | will not pay the Estimate or Appraisal until the panel or part is replaced using the approved OEM procedure. |

**Parent topic:** [Panel sectioning](../topics/294CC29C4268E7E1E761F735A712AEE8.html "Panel sectioning is a repair operation that involves replacing a section of a panel, rather than an entire panel. It is allowed only when a safe, quality repair is possible. When a repair facility performs a panel section repair, an appropriate time applies to the Estimate or Appraisal.")
