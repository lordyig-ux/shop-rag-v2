# Vehicle teardown operations

List of operations that are performed with a vehicle teardown.

### Teardown operations

Teardown estimates may involve operations that are sometimes performed separately.

| If the Operation is … | Then the cost is … |
| --- | --- |
| air conditioning (A/C) evacuation | included as a teardown payment if the cost is incurred to facilitate teardown. |
| unibody/frame measurements approved or requested by ICBC to determine the repair costs | the time agreed to perform the measuring and/or set up on a four point anchoring system. It is paid as part of the teardown time. |
