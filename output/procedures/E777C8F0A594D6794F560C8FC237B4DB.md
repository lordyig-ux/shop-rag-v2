# Preferred rental vehicle service features

In a location where a rental vehicle provider is the preferred provider, the company offers special features on all rental vehicles.

### Vehicle and service features

Customers can choose to use any rental vehicle provider, but claim representatives recommend a preferred rental vehicle supplier to the customer, provided one is available in the customer's area.

Rental vehicle features ensure that the customer receives superior service and high-quality vehicles. The table shows the vehicle and service features.

Remember: A winter tire, per the Ministry of Transportation and Transit's [Winter Tires in BC Handout (pdf)](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/driving-and-transportation/driving/winter/pdfs/wintertiresinbc_handout.pdf), includes two types of tires allowed on [Designated Winter Tire and Chain Routes](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/driving-and-cycling/traveller-information/seasonal/winter-driving/winter-tire-and-chain-up-routes).

Customers who request "severe winter condition (mountain snowflake)" or "all-season mud and snow (M+S)" tires on their rental vehicle

* should have the same type of tires on their own vehicle (talk to your manager about exceptions), and
* may exhaust their Loss of Use (LOU) coverage faster if the rental vehicle provider has an additional fee.

Tip: Most rental vehicle providers already include all-season tires.
