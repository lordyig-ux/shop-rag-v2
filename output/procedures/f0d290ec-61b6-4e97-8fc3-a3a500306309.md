# Invoice for storage facility access

The applicable charges to invoice, and invoice requirements for Storage Facility Access (SFA).

### Applicable charges

An ICBC representative may need to attend a tow supplier storage facility yard and/or building to

* view and/or take photographs of a vehicle
* estimate the vehicle damage
* collect a vehicle's licence plates, and/or
* remove personal belongings from a vehicle.

The tow supplier storage facility yard is permitted to charge ICBC a Storage Facility Access (SFA) fee on a [Towing Invoice (CL14C)](http://icbcwebapps/corpforms/formspdf/CL14C.pdf) form.

### SFA fee invoice requirements

The SFA fee invoice requirements includes

* one day of storage charged at the
  + designated supplier business type
  + applicable zone, and
  + applicable vehicle daily storage rate
* each vehicle being viewed and/or estimated by ICBC on site at the tow supplier storage facility yard and/or building, and
* a standalone item, itemized as SFA in the "No. of Days" field on the CL14C form.
