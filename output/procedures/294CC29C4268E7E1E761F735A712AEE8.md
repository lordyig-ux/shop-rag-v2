# Panel sectioning

Panel sectioning is a repair operation that involves replacing a section of a panel, rather than an entire panel. It is allowed only when a safe, quality repair is possible. When a repair facility performs a panel section repair, an appropriate time applies to the Estimate or Appraisal.

### Apply estimate amounts

| When a panel section ... | and the section ... | Then the estimator ... |
| --- | --- | --- |
| is available:  * Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) * pre-priced, or * recycled | is listed in Mitchell Cloud Estimating (MCE) | enters the appropriate time from MCE on the Estimate or Appraisal. |
| is available only as a  * recycled part, or * a whole new part | is **not** listed in MCE | applies a reasonable judgment time. |
