# Freight and crating charges applied to recycled parts

Guidelines for applying freight and crating charges to recycled parts.

### Freight and crating charges

All recycled parts must include any freight or handing charges listed on the recycled parts locater. When the part is received by the collision repair program facility, the supplier invoice may have the freight and crating charges listed as a separate charge line on the invoice, however the invoice price must match what was listed on the locater.

In the event that the charges are listed on separate charge lines on the supplier invoice, ensure the part price is adjusted on the estimate to reflect the invoice part price. Add any freight or crating charges to the estimate using the "freight" line from "My template/01 Standard" in Mitchell Cloud Estimating (MCE).

Note: The part price and additional charges detailed on the supplier invoice cannot exceed the original inventoried part price from the Car-Part inventory.
