# Approve commercial vehicle cash settlement

Who is responsible for approving the different commercial vehicle cash settlements.

### Prerequisites

Claim involves a commercial vehicle.

### Context

Who to obtain a cash settlement approval from for the commercial vehicle.

### Approval process

The approval process uses the cash settlement amount, and claim type to determine who will authorize the cash settlement.

**Cash settlement amount**:

Use the table to identify the level of authorization required for a cash settlement.

| Cash settlements | Authorizer |
| --- | --- |
| Under $5,000 | Appraiser |
| $5,000 to $10,000 | Appraiser manager |
| Over $10,000 | Operations manager |

**Claim type**:

In addition to the cash settlement amount, the applicable manager must also approve of a cash settlement when the claim is a:

* hit and run claim, or
* audio, visual, and communication equipment claim.

Note: All cash settlements must meet the requirements outlined in the claim procedures.
