# Repairs of load-bearing components and structures policy

Policy on repairs of load-bearing components and structures.

### Eligibility

The policy applies to vehicles and machinery that:

* are constructed to be load-bearing (such as a crane or forklift), or
* have load-bearing equipment attached to them (such as a rollover cage attached to a tractor).

### Policy Detail

When a structural load-bearing component, designed to resist defined loads, is damaged by direct physical damage, the repair must be tested and re-certified by one of the following:

* a professional engineer, or
* original equipment manufacturer.

Important: When a claim involves such a situation, the ICBC representative must contact the exposure owner, as they provide instruction or, in certain situations, take over handling the claim.

### Rationale for policy

Structural load-bearing components are designed to absorb or resist a specific, defined load or force. The requirement for testing and recertification ensures the component or structure is returned to a condition capable of carrying out its original design function with an adequate margin of safety.
