# Maximum taxi van conversion allowance

Guidelines for applying the maximum taxi van conversion allowance to a total loss van.

### Maximum allowance

The maximum conversion allowance for a total loss van equipped to transport disabled passengers is as follows:

| When the van... | Then... |
| --- | --- |
| was converted under the old price structure | a maximum conversion allowance of $4,100 applies. |
| was converted by Mahadev Metalworks | a maximum conversion allowance of $8,500 applies. |
| is a Ford Motor Company Liberty van, already converted for wheelchair accessibility | the cost of this conversion must be obtained from the local Ford dealership. |
