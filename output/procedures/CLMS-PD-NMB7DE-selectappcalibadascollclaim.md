# Select the appropriate calibration for an advanced driver assistance system in a collision claim

The collision repair program participant may need to calibrate cameras or radar units for vehicles equipped with advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS) that have a collision claim.

### When this applies

When an ADAS system requires calibration as part of the repair.

Note: For more information, refer to the topic, [Advanced driver assistance systems calibrations for collision claims](CLMS-PL-FEBKJL-adascalibrationscollisonclaim.html "The collision repair program participant may need to calibrate cameras or radar units for vehicles equipped with advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS) that are involved in a collision claim.")

### Procedure

1. Select the appropriate estimate entry from the My Templates dropdown list in Mitchell Cloud Estimating (MCE).

   | If the calibration is for a ... | and the calibration is ... | then the participant must ... |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | forward facing camera or radar unit | sublet to another collision or glass program participant to complete | * select an auto-populated sublet ADAS calibration type from My Templates, including   + ADAS CALIBRATION STATIC   + ADAS CALIBRATION DYNAMIC, or   + ADAS CALIBRATION UNIVERSAL |
   | sublet to a non-participant program participant to complete | * select an auto-populated sublet ADAS calibration type from My Templates, including   + ADAS CALIBRATION STATIC   + ADAS CALIBRATION DYNAMIC, or   + ADAS CALIBRATION UNIVERSAL * change the dollar amount to reflect the sublet amount before taxes, and * add an explanation line outlining the type of calibration performed, including the additional labour operations, as required, to support the additional amount billed. Note: For more information refer to the topic, [Sublet repair](5C79B30A648336E3A4514EFDB5C73405.html "Definition of a sublet repair.") |
   | completed in-house by a collision repair program participant | * select an auto-populated sublet ADAS calibration type from My Templates, including   + ADAS CALIBRATION STATIC   + ADAS CALIBRATION DYNAMIC, or   + ADAS CALIBRATION UNIVERSAL, and * convert the appropriate amount to labour hours. Note: For more information how to convert sublet into flat rate labour time refer to the topic,[Sublet conversion to Alternative Transportation Service hourly compensation](8430AAA8A85F2F4DC96DCE3055BB2A49.html "Guidelines for sublet conversion to Alternative Transportation Service (ATS) hourly compensation.") |
   | not forward facing camera or radar unit | * sublet to another program participant or non-program facility to complete, or * completed in-house | * select the applicable sublet ADAS calibration from My Templates, including   + REAR CROSS TRAFFIC ALERT   + LANE KEEPING ASSIST   + ADAPTIVE CRUISE CONTROL   + AUTOMATIC BRAKING   + 360 DEGREE VIEW   + REARVIEW CAMERA, or   + OTHER ADAS CALIBRATIONS * enter the dollar amount to reflect the sublet amount before taxes, and * add an explanation line outlining the type of calibration performed including additional labour operations as required to support the additional amount billed. Note: Sublet can be converted into labour hours only when ICBC has a pre-stored amount. |

   Note: Documentation supporting OEM repair procedures and operations performed as outlined in the Collision repair program guide, must be retained when the vehicle has ADAS.
2. Perform the calibration.
