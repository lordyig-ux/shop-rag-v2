# Standards of performance for alternative parts suppliers on Original Equipment manufacturer surplus parts

Guidelines for alternative parts suppliers on Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) surplus parts must meet the standards of performance for all types of parts, and to OEM surplus parts.

### Standards of performance

OEM surplus parts suppliers must meet these and all general standards of performance:

| Requirement | Standard of performance |
| --- | --- |
| Price guarantee | OEM surplus parts pricing listed on the Estimate or Appraisal must be used by the listed part supplier for a period of 30 days from the date the estimate was written. |
| Part type | All parts must be manufactured by the OEM. |
