# Select Vancouver Island rental rates

ICBC replacement vehicle daily rental rates for select Vancouver Island locations (Enterprise Rent-A-Car) including Comox, Courtney, Duncan, and Nanaimo.

Important: Review the [Partner disclaimer for rental vehicles](CLMS-RF-JL5TM7-partner-disclaimer-rental-veh.html "When considering the customer's rental vehicle needs, the customer's policy (including limits with respect to rental coverage) needs to be reviewed.").

### Daily rental rates

The following vehicle classes are available for the corresponding daily rental rates.

View [Rental vehicle makes and models](CLMS-RF-MANJGF-rental-vehicle-makes-models.html "Examples of vehicle makes and models, grouped by vehicle class, and whether the preferred rental supplier may offer it as a rental vehicle.") to determine the applicable vehicle class.
