# Sheet molding compound panel allowance

Definition of sheet molding compound (SMC) panel.

### Definition

SMC panels are an alternative to sheet metal panels. They require the use of an adhesive for repairs. ICBC covers the cost of the adhesive required to bond Sheet Molding Compound (SMC) panels. The rate is $3.00 per linear foot of panel bond area.
