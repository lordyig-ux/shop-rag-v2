# Select a tow company from the tow rotation list

Steps for using the claim centre tow rotation list to select a tow company.

### Prerequisites

Before you select a tow company from the tow rotation list, you must

* identify the tow type as non-captive, and
* identify the tow destination.

### When this applies

When a tow is a non-captive tow, use the claim centre tow rotation list to select a tow company to perform the vehicle tow.

A tow rotation list is created and managed by the Supplier Programs & Administration department.

### Procedure

1. Select the next available tow company on the tow rotation list.
2. Determine whether the selected tow company is able to meet the tow service requirements.

   | If the selected tow company is ... | Then ... |
   | --- | --- |
   | able to meet tow service requirements | continue with the tow arrangements. |
   | unable to meet tow service requirements | assign the tow request to the next company on the tow rotation list. |

   Note: For more information about tow service requirements, refer to the topic, Tow service delay requirements.
