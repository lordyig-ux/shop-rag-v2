# Determine coverage for an uninsured parked vehicle

A claim owner or exposure owner determines if coverage is available for damage to an uninsured vehicle that was parked when struck by an ICBC insured vehicle. For claims with a date of loss (DOL) on May 1, 2021, or later.

### Prerequisites

The registered owner (RO) or lessee of an uninsured vehicle has reported vehicle damage to their parked vehicle, as a result of being struck by the driver of the third party vehicle that is insured by ICBC.

### When this applies

When a RO or lessee reports a claim for damage to their uninsured parked vehicle, a claim owner or exposure owner must determine if coverage is available for the parked uninsured vehicle by confirming

* location, that is, was it [on-highway or off-highway](https://procedures/claims/DitaImages/MV7521-highway-definition-guide.pdf), and
* responsibility.

### Procedure

1. Determine if the loss location was on a highway.

   Note: As per [section 173 of the Insurance (Vehicle) Act](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96231_01#section173), the vehicle damage lawsuit ban does not apply to a claim occurring off highway, if the crash does not involve at least two licensed and insured standard motor vehicles.

   | If the loss was ... | Then ... |
   | --- | --- |
   | on-highway | no coverage is available for the uninsured parked vehicle damage, due to the lawsuit ban. Advise the customer that no coverage is available.  Procedure ends here. |
   | off-highway | coverage may be available. Proceed to step 2. |
2. Determine responsibility, and advise the customer of the results.

   Note: For additional information, refer to [Responsibility assessments](Topic.aspx?rootmap=INVN-MP-TK4G61-investigation&topic=CLMS-RF-ST4FJR-responsibility-assessments "Responsibility assessments are a result of a or doing a detailed review of the crash to decide who is responsible, and to what degree. Most claims require a responsibility assessment.").

   | If the motorist is ... | Then ... |
   | --- | --- |
   | responsible | the uninsured parked vehicle damage has coverage from the responsible motorist's Third Party Liability coverage. |
   | not responsible | no coverage is available for the uninsured parked vehicle damage, |

   Note: The RO or lessee of an uninsured vehicle parked off highway, may have a Third Party Liability claim against the responsible vehicle that struck their vehicle, if the third party vehicle was licensed and insured when they struck the uninsured parked vehicle.
3. Check if the third party has reported a claim for this loss.

   | If a third party (responsible motorist) ... | Then ... |
   | --- | --- |
   | reported | * associate the third party's claim to the RO or lessee's parked uninsured vehicle claim, and * use the procedure, [Create an Event-related association](Topic.aspx?rootmap=ClaimCenter-user-guide&topic=CLMS-PD-ELJRAW-eventrelated-association "Create an Event-related association when associating two or more British Columbia (B.C.) vehicle claims and or statutory (STAT) claims.").  Note: For additional information, refer to [Associations](Topic.aspx?rootmap=ClaimCenter-user-guide&topic=CCUG-CO-654ASS "Create and manage relationships between claims, using association types.") |
   | did not report | [start a Statutory claim](Topic.aspx?rootmap=ClaimCenter-user-guide&topic=start-claim-statutory "Start a claim as a statutory (STAT) claim when the customer does not have ICBC insurance coverage.") for the RO or lessee's parked uninsured vehicle. |
4. [open a Towing - No Own Damage exposure](Topic.aspx?rootmap=ClaimCenter-user-guide&topic=CCUG-PD-TNG204 "Open a Towing - No Own Damage exposure when a customer does not have Own Damage Coverage with ICBC or a private insurer and ICBC must assist them with their vehicle repairs, settlement or towing."), and set the reserves for the uninsured parked vehicle claim.

   Note: The portion of the vehicle repair or replacement that the responsible motorist is not responsible for, is
   * paid against the Towing - No Own Damage exposure, and
   * transferred to the responsible motorist's Third Party Liability exposure by financial distribution.
