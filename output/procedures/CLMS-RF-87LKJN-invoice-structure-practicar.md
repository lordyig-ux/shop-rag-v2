# Invoicing structure of the preferred supplier agreement between ICBC and Practicar Car and Truck Rentals

Under direct rental invoicing, vehicle availability is guaranteed with no-charge vehicle size upgrades when the appropriate vehicle size is unavailable.

Practicar will

* make best efforts to have the other vehicle classes described in Schedule 1 and Schedule 2 available during the course of normal business
* include
  + manufacturer provided toll-free roadside assistance, and
  + unlimited daily kilometers for all rental vehicles, and
* provide
  + a pick-up and drop-off service to the renter
  + after-hour drop boxes at all Practicar locations, and
  + vehicle delivery services and vehicle drop-off at the repair facility, if requested.

The following areas are subject to a flat rate pick-up or delivery charge:

* Christina Lake: $70
* Grand Forks: $25
* Greenwood: $80
* Salmo $50
