# Recycled glass clean-up and testing

Summary of recycled glass clean-up allowances and testing requirements.

### Clean up allowance

Recycled glass must be repaired, tested and cleaned before installation on the vehicle.

The table presents the allowance to repair, test, and clean recycled glass:

| Glass type | Allowance |
| --- | --- |
| Windshield or back glass | $20.00 |
| Side glass (excluding vent glass) | $15.00 |
| Total clean-up allowance when more than one glass is being replaced. | $30.00 |

### Testing heated glass requirements

To ensure proper operation of the heating elements, heated glass must be tested by the:

* recycler prior to delivery, and
* glass facility prior to installation.

### Clean-up fee

Glass Web Express (GWE) includes the appropriate clean-up fee automatically when recycled glass is selected. When pricing different glass options, clean-up fees must be considered as part of the overall cost of the recycled glass in any comparison with new glass.
