# Warranty on alternative parts

Summary of warranties on alternative part types.

### Warranty

The warranty on an alternative part depends upon the type of part utilized.

The warranty for each type of part is presented in the table.

| Type of part | Warranty |
| --- | --- |
| Aftermarket | Lifetime warranty for as long as the original owner owns the vehicle. |
| Alloy wheel | Lifetime warranty for as long as the original owner owns the vehicle. This applies to the following:   * repaired wheels, and * recycled wheels.   Note: The refinish application has a warranty of one year. |
| Exchange part | Lifetime warranty for as long as the original owner owns the vehicle. This applies to the following:   * exchange bumpers * reinforcement bars, and * other exchange parts. |
| Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) surplus | one year. |
| radiators and air conditioning parts | Lifetime warranty for as long as the original owner owns the vehicle. |

### General limit of warranty

The parts supplier is not responsible for failure due to

* improper installation
* accident
* misuse
* abuse, or
* improper maintenance.

### Additional limits to warranty

The following specific limits apply over and above general warranty limits:

| Type of part | The parts supplier is not responsible for failure due to ... |
| --- | --- |
| Alloy wheel repair | wheel weights. |
| Exchange parts | * warping, other than premature warping, or * any subsequent repair or modification. |

### Failure during warranty period

Where a failure occurs to a part within the applicable warranty period, the parts supplier must

* replace the part, or
* refund the purchase price of that part to the repair facility that
  + purchased the part, or
  + replaced the defective partincluding
  + freight, and
  + all reasonable labour charges for removal and replacement, and where applicable, refinishing.
