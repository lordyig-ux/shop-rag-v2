# Optional glass parts to a glass estimate

Glass Web Express (GWE) provides information on glass-related parts and labour and acceptable exceptions.

### Specific Glass operations

Glass related items are captured differently in GWE depending on the
item. It is important to capture all items accurately.

| Part or labour operation | How to capture |
| --- | --- |
| Remove and install (R & I) external sun visors | Select from the R & I items list. |
| After market tinting | Enter the appropriate parts and labour cost for tinting in the Other field in the R & I items. |
| After market rain sensor gel pads (Allowance) | Select Rain Sensor Gel Pad Allowance from the R & I parts menu. |
| Remove and install vinyl tops to facilitate glass work | Enter times and rate in the Other field in the R & I items list. |

### Acceptable exception items

Only certain items are acceptable exception items. Some acceptable exception items require prior approval from Provincial Glass Operations (PGO).

| Type of operations or parts involved | Approval |
| --- | --- |
| Cost of glass and labour time for flat laminated windshields, also referred to as flat glass. | Not required. |
| * Moldings   + belt   + sash, and   + scalp * interior trim * glass run channels * window regulator components * windshield wiper arms and blades * second piece of glass * Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) clips, and * Sublet Advanced Driver Assist System (ADAS) calibrations. | Required. |
