# Commercial refinish times for components

List of commercial refinish times for components.

### Included items

A refinishing labour time applies to each different component of a commercial vehicle.

Components include:

* bumpers
* hoods
* roof fairings
* cab extenders
* sun visors
* fuel tank fairings
* steel wheels
* chassis, and
* miscellaneous items

Refer to the [ICBC Heavy Equipment (Truck) Paint Chart](https://www.icbc.com/partners/material-damage/Documents/heavy-equipment-paint-chart.pdf) for applicable refinish times.
