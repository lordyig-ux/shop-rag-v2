# Permanently attached equipment

Definition of permanently attached equipment.

### Definition

Permanently attached means affixed to a permanently attached part of the vehicle,

* attached using specified attachment methods, and
* requiring use of a tool for attachment and/or removal.

### Requirements to qualify as permanently attached

Requirements to qualify as permanently attached are as follows:

| Requirement | Acceptable methods/tools | Unacceptable methods/tools |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attachment method | * bolts * screws * rivets * cements * fluxes * cotter pin that requires bending with a tool in order to both secure and remove * heat, or * locks that require keys or combinations. | * Velcro-type fasteners * locks (other than those requiring keys or combinations) * wing nuts * wires * ropes or strings * snap fasteners * suction devices * hooks * magnets * buttons * pins * vice grips * clamps, or * chains (whether permanently attached to the vehicle, or not). |
| Attachment or removal tools | * wrenches * screw drivers * drills * pliers * acetylene torches * blow torches * steamers, or * solvents. | * by hand |
