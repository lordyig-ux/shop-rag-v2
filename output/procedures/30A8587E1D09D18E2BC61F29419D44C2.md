# Sublet repairs on commercial claims

Guidelines on subletting repairs on commercial claims.

### Applicable rate

When a commercial vehicle sublets a portion of a repair to an automobile collision repair facility, then the applicable automobile body labour rate and refinish rate apply, based on the repairer completing the sublet repairs.

### Example

When the commercial/heavy equipment vehicle sublet repairs are completed at a collision repair program participant, then the program participant rates apply.

### Estimate requirements

When the repair facility requests a separate Estimate for the sublet work, the estimator or appraiser creates a separate Estimate for them.
