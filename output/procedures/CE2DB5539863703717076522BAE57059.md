# Suppliers going out of business

Policy on ICBC suppliers that are going out of business.

### Eligibility

ICBC suppliers must advise ICBC as soon as possible when a supplier is going out of business.

This policy applies to any supplier going out of business.

### Policy

Suppliers going out of business must advise their assigned claims office immediately.

### Rationale

This ensures that there is no misuse of the supplier number.

### Terminate supplier number

After the supplier informs ICBC that they are going out of business, they must terminate the supplier number of a supplier by:

* Estimating Services Manager contacts the Accreditation department, and

* Accreditation department terminates the supplier number.
