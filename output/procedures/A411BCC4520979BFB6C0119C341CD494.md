# Alternative Transportation Service replacement vehicle criteria

Collision repair program participants offering Alternative Transportation Service (ATS) replacement vehicles must meet specific criteria.

### ATS replacement vehicle criteria

The vehicle offered for use under the ATS must meet specific criteria regarding age, safety, and appearance.

ATS replacement vehicles must meet the following criteria.

| Type of replacement vehicle | Criteria |
| --- | --- |
| Participant owned or leased | Vehicles must be  * no older than eight years * a mid-size vehicle, at minimum * maintained in a safe and roadworthy condition, and * clean and presentable. |
| Rental company supplied | Vehicles are arranged through a business arrangement between the participant and the rental company. |

A participant may provide an ATS replacement vehicle utilizing a rental company’s vehicle at the participant’s expense. The participant must upload documentation to support that a vehicle was provided to the customer, such as a signed customer rental agreement or Temporary Substitute Motor Vehicle agreement, according to the documentation requirements listed in Estimate and Supporting Documentation Requirements section of the [Collision Repair Program guide](https://partners.icbc.com/assets/2NgmPKP5LdEwCPfTWbCP8F/collision-program-guide.pdf).
