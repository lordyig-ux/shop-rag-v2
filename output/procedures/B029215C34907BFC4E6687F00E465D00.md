# ICBC salvage facilities in British Columbia

List of ICBC salvage facilities in British Columbia including their hours of operation, address, ship to/location codes, and phone number.

### Salvage facilities

Each ICBC facility is assigned one of six salvage facilities to use for total loss vehicle tows. Claims staff use the salvage facility list to find the salvage facility that is located in the ICBC facility area.

Hours of operation for all salvage facilities are 8:30 am to 4:30 pm, Monday to Friday.

### Off-site storage and salvage sales facilities

Salvage operations has created partnerships with multiple towing and storage facilities across BC. These facilities allow ICBC to store and sell salvage from their locations to reduce towing expenses and alleviate capacity issues. Salvage operations publish and maintain a list of approved off-site salvage facilities which they share with claims administration who coordinate the movement of vehicles that have been deemed a total loss.
