# Frame rate application to unibody vehicle repairs

Summary of frame rate application to uni-body vehicle repairs.

### Required equipment use for frame rate

Frame repairs require the use of special equipment, and due to the complexity and the equipment involved, pulling and aligning frames is paid at a frame rate when specific repair equipment is used.

For the frame rate to apply, the repair facility must use specific equipment to perform the repair, including

* an anchoring system, such as P-4’s, bench or similar system, to secure the vehicle at a minimum of four points
* simultaneous three dimensional electronic measuring equipment with data subscription maintaining system updates upon request, and
* a hydraulic pulling equipment capable of multiple pulls at any angle.

Note: Please refer to the MD Guide, Estimating and Supporting Documentation Requirements for pre- and post-repair electronic three dimensional measuring reports.

Estimate repair entries that auto populate with a frame rate from Mitchel Estimating should not be adjusted.

### When frame rate applies

Repair time(s) for the following procedures is paid at the repair facility’s applicable frame rate

* set up
* measuring
* pulling
* aligning
* cutting, and
* welding of access holes.

Important: All requirements for frame rate must be met for the frame rate to apply.

### Unacceptable billing

Billing at the frame rate when all of the required equipment was not used or necessary during the repair process, may result in a quality assurance (QA) assessment.
