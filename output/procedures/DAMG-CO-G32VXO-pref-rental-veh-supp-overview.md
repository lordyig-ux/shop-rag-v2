# Preferred rental vehicle suppliers overview

Preferred rental vehicle suppliers offer ICBC customers enhanced benefits and features when renting a substitute vehicle.

ICBC always recommends the preferred rental vehicle provider for the customer's location; however, the customer can choose the provider they want.
