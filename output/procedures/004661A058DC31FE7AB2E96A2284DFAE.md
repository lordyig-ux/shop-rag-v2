# Towing and Recovery Plus supplier requirements

Summary of requirements the towing and recovery plus suppliers must have for their storage yard(s) and/or building(s) prior to qualifying as an ICBC towing and recovery plus supplier.

### Requirements

Towing and recovery plus suppliers must meet and maintain specific requirements to be qualified.

Note: Failure to meet all requirements may disqualify a supplier from Towing and Recovery Plus.

| When the requirement is… | The supplier must… |
| --- | --- |
| vehicle categories | be capable of towing and recovering all vehicles up to and including Category II. |
| storage facility office area | * have an office at the main storage facility, that is staffed during regular business hours, and * provide a photograph of the office to ICBC. |
| additional storage yard access | provide customer access to their vehicle within one hour during normal business hours for any secondary storage yard(s)/building(s). |
| digital camera | have cameras with:   * minimum 1.3 megapixels * capable of 640 x 480 resolution, and * have a macro option. |
| electronic dispatching | * have a designated dispatch email address, or * dispatch fax number for receiving the External Tow Dispatch Request (WEB1075) from ICBC. |
| invoicing | be equipped to submit all invoices electronically to ICBC. |
| tow vehicle list | provide electronically the current licensed and registered towing vehicles to ICBC on an annual basis. |
| uniforms | * have employees wear professional uniforms displaying the company name, and * provide a photograph of the uniform to ICBC.   Note: Uniforms to be supplied by the company. |
| exterior lightening | * have their own exterior lighting which illuminates the storage yard(s) and/or building(s), and * provide safe passage when navigating in the storage yard(s)/building(s). |
| storage capacity | have the vehicle storage capacity to be four to one (4 spaces for each tow vehicle).  Note: This requirement excludes motorcycles. |
