# Sublet towing service urgent and non-urgent recoveries

Guidelines for sublet towing services requiring an urgent or non-urgent recovery

### Services

The table presents the sublet towing services for urgent or non-urgent recoveries.

Note: Urgent recovery is defined as an instance where there is insufficient time to complete a claim review by Material Damage Services.

| Recovery types | Sublet towing service |
| --- | --- |
| Urgent | Tow company handles the direct arrangements for the sublet services through:  * invoicing for the required sublet services, and * applying the 15% markup to the invoiced net sublet amount charges, subject to the trade discount policy. |
| Non-urgent | ICBC:  * may respond to the recovery, and * reserves the right to advise, in advance of the tow company, who is securing sublet services or equipment to the crash location. Note: ICBC is authorized to arrange, negotiate directly, and pay for the service or equipment. |
| * Non-urgent, and * ICBC does not handle the direct billing of the sublet services or equipment. | Tow company:  * arranges the required services or rental equipment, and * applies the 15% markup to the invoiced net sublet amount charges,subject to the trade discount policy. |
