# Standards of performance for repair facilities on alloy wheel repair

Guidelines for repair facilities on alloy wheel repairs.

### Standards of performance

The provincial government passed regulations governing the weld repair of aluminum alloy wheels.

Alloy wheel repairers must meet all these and all general standards of performance:

| Requirement | Standard of performance |
| --- | --- |
| Regulation compliance | Each repair facility must comply with   * the Motor Vehicle Act Regulations and * these procedures. |
| Required identification | * Each repair must bear the appropriate identification as outlined in the Motor Vehicle Act Regulations. * The repairer must ensure this identification is legible. |
| Repair vs replace decision | The decision on repair vs. replace rests with the repair facility. The repairer or contractor must notify ICBC if the wheel is not repairable. |
| Recycled/Repaired wheel price list | Approved repairers must supply claims offices with a price list of available, in-stock recycled or repaired wheels. |
| Response time | Repairers must make every effort to reduce delivery time in an emergency situation. |
| Repair rates | Repairers’ charges to ICBC for alloy wheel repairs must be based on the rates set by ICBC. |

### Applicable legislations

Refer to the Motor Vehicle Act Regulations, Division, 7.09, item 20 of the schedule.
