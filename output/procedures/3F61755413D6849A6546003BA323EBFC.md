# Negate vehicle damage

Steps to negate vehicle damage that does not impact the value of the damaged parts.

### When this applies

Estimators should negate vehicle damage that is not significant in relation to the overall integrity of the damaged part.

### Procedure

1. Identify damage that can be negated.
2. Discuss the damage with the insured:

   * explain the meaning of "negate"
   * note the reason(s) for negating the damage, and
   * reach agreement on what damage is being negated.
3. Enter 0.0 under the appropriate operation hours.
4. Add a line edit remark "damage negated".
