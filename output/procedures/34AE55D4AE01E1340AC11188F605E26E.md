# Provide tow instructions to the tow company

Steps to contact a tow company, and what information to provide.

### When this applies

Tow companies require a set of instructions which include details about a vehicle before the vehicle is towed.

### Procedure

1. Contact the tow company 

   Tow companies may be contacted by

   * e-mail
   * External Tow Dispatch Request form (WEB1075)
   * fax
   * phone, or
   * tow company internal system.
2. Provide the following information: 

   * tow requestor and resource number
   * vehicle location
   * claim number
   * tow origin and destination
   * date of loss
   * customer name, and
   * vehicle
     + year
     + make
     + model
     + color, and
     + VIN.
