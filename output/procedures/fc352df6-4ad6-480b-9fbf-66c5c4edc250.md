# Vehicle decals and wraps

Definition of full/partial vehicle decals, panel decals, and vehicle wraps.

### Definition

Vehicle decals and wraps are designed for:

* vehicle detailing
* graphic decorating
* advertising, and
* protecting paint surface.

### Types of decals and vehicle wraps

| Types of vehicle coverings | Features |
| --- | --- |
| Decal (full/partial/panel) | * Applied to either a single panel, multiple panels, or the full vehicle * Specified by the panel or vehicle * Can be removable, but are generally designed as permanent. |
| Wrap | * Typically larger and more modern in design than panel decals * Generally applied to the full vehicle * Either removable or permanent |

Note: Own Damage coverage is provided for permanent/removable decals and vehicle wraps that meet the definition of permanently attached equipment defined in [Permanently attached equipment](https://procedures/claims/Pages/topic.aspx?rootmap=DAMG-MP-NRP91J-damage&topic=331C6EC250056AC29BAFC8C47CCBA1BE&dm=Customer%20and%20Vehicle%20Services). Tools, such as a heat gun and/or solvents, are necessary for its removal. Wraps or coverings of a temporary nature that do not require a tool for removal are not covered as permanently attached equipment of a vehicle.
