# Specialty Vehicle Appraisal unit assignment policy

Policy on when a total loss must be assigned to the Specialty Vehicle Appraisal (SVA) unit.

### Policy

Estimators must assign the estimating activity to the specialty vehicle appraisal (SVA) unit when

* handling a claim on a vehicle with a Special Risk Own Damage Policy (APV274)
* handling a claim on a vehicle with a model year of 1973 or older where coverage is provided under ICBC's
  + Collector Motor Vehicle Agreed Value Policy (APV348) - Actual Cash Value
  + Modified Collector Vehicle Policy (MV1437) - Declared Value, or
  + Vintage Motor Vehicle Certificate (APV44) - Stated Value, and
* the vehicle

  + meets specialty vehicle criteria as provided in [Vehicle list for the specialty vehicle appraisal (SVA) unit](DAMG-RF-172L75-vehicle-list-criteria-sva-unit.html "The specialty vehicle appraisal (SVA) unit review and process estimates for all vehicles listed, regardless of the chosen repair facility in the Collision Repair Program."), and
  + is potentially a total loss.

Note: To warrant assignment to the SVA unit, the vehicle must meet SVA vehicle criteria or have one of the aforementioned policies. For example, a Tesla is on the SVA criteria list, but would not have one of the listed policies. Refer to Additional Coverages for information on collector or vintage vehicle policies.
