# Invoicing structure of the preferred supplier agreement between ICBC and Budget Car and Truck Rentals

Under direct rental invoicing, vehicle availability is guaranteed with no-charge vehicle size upgrades when appropriate vehicle size is unavailable.

Budget Car and Truck Rentals (Budget) will

* accept vehicle drop-off at any Budget location in BC at no additional charge to ICBC
* ensure all Budget locations have after-hours drop boxes available
* guarantee vehicle availability; during major events 24 hours notice may be required
* permit renters to travel to the USA with the rental vehicle, and
* provide
  + a pick-up and drop-off service to the renter within one hour of rental assignment
  + all pick-up and drop-off services within 30 kilometres at no charge to ICBC, and
  + vehicle delivery services and vehicle drop-off at the repair facility, if requested.
