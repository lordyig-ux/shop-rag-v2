# Total loss settlement dispute policy

The original claim owner assigned to settle a customer's total loss must also handle any resulting total loss settlement disputes raised by the customer.

### Applicability

When a customer disputes their total loss settlement amount, the original claim owner must

* discuss the settlement with the customer to address their questions or concerns, and
* obtain their manager's review of the settlement upon the customer's request for a review.
