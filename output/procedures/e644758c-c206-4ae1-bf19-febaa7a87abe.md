# Part mark-up policy

When ICBC authorizes a part mark-up on a Estimate or Appraisal form.

### Policy

ICBC authorizes the following part mark-up on claims.

| When the available price … | There is no ... |
| --- | --- |
| is discounted for less than retail price | no mark-up. |
| is not discounted from the retail price to any buyers | a 10% mark-up. |

Note: The participant can mark up to 10 percent on the price of parts, when

* parts are not available for less than retail price, and
* it is replaced due to other repairs completed at the repair facility.

Note: Parts pricing on the estimate may be below retail pricing, but not below parts suppliers or participants cost.
