# Preferred rental vehicle customer requirements

Customers renting substitute vehicles must meet certain requirements set by preferred rental vehicle companies.

### Requirements

Each preferred rental vehicle company has certain requirements for customers.

| Rental Company | Requirements of customer |
| --- | --- |
| Budget Car and Truck Rentals | * Provide a pre-authorized deposit in the minimum amount of $100 on a major credit card in the customer's name. * Be a minimum of 18 years of age. * Have insurance on their own vehicle which can transfer to the rental vehicle. |
| Enterprise Rent-A-Car | * Provide a pre-authorized deposit in the minimum amount of $100 on a major credit card in the customer's name. * Be a minimum of 18 years of age (25 or older to rent 12- and 15-passenger vans). * Have insurance on their own vehicle which can transfer to the rental vehicle. |
| PractiCar Car and Truck Rentals | * Provide a pre-authorized deposit in the minimum amount of $500 on a major credit card in the customer's name. * Be 19 years of age or older. * Have insurance on their own vehicle which can transfer to the rental vehicle. |
