# Estimator requirements for recycled parts

Requirements for the estimator to use recycled parts on customer’s vehicle.

### Requirements

The table presents the requirements the estimator must meet when using recycled parts on a customer’s vehicle.

| Requirement | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Part is available | Estimator researches the availability of the recycled part with the Car-Part.com integrated search tool, ipro/Car-Part-pro, within Mitchell Cloud Estimating (MCE). |
| Defined part | The defined part must include the vehicle:  * year * make * model * body style, and * vehicle identification number (VIN).   The additional information may be requested by the recycled part supplier:   * engine size * colour, or trim identification codes, * options and series.   The estimator must confirm before sending out a parts request:   * whether there is a Replacement Cost Endorsement (APV286A) or New Vehicle Replacement Plus (NVR+) policy in effect, and * the make, model, trim level, body style and options of the vehicle |
| Economical to use | Determines whether the recycled part will result in a more economical repair than with a new part. If no, then the estimator may:  * renegotiate the price with the supplier * find another supplier, or * consider using alternative and/or exchange Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) parts.   Note: Complete orders are given higher priority than incomplete orders. |
