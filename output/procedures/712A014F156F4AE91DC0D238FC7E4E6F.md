# Standards of performance for repair facilities on all parts

Guidelines for standards of performance on all parts for repair facilities.

### Standards of performance

All repair facilities must meet all these standards of performance, regardless of the work being performed:

| Requirement | Standard of performance |
| --- | --- |
| Parts identification | All parts requests must be properly identified by   * Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) * year * make * model * body style, and/or * physical description.   Note: The parts suppliers may also require the Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) part number at the time of the initial order. |
| Parts check | The repair facility must visually inspect all parts before work begins to ensure they are acceptable.  Note: If a problem exists, the repair facility must phone the supplier directly. |
| Attempt to use parts | The repair facility must make a reasonable attempt to use each part before phoning the supplier with a quality or fit issue. |
| Administration and ordering | Repair facilities must   * order all parts by ICBC claim number, and * attach a copy of the original invoice, showing the ICBC claim number, to every repair account submitted for payment. |

### Standards are not met

If a repair facility chooses to use a supplier or a part that does not meet the standards of performance, the repair facility and the parts supplier assume all responsibility for the quality of the part and workmanship.
