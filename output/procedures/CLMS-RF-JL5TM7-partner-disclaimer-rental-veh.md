# Partner disclaimer for rental vehicles

When considering the customer's rental vehicle needs, the customer's policy (including limits with respect to rental coverage) needs to be reviewed.

Advise the customer that the rental vehicle will be in a comparable class (for example, make, model, or size) to the vehicle they own. It is within ICBC's discretion to determine the type of rental vehicle.

ICBC rental rate documents contain examples of vehicle makes and models that correspond with rental rates by vehicle class. There may be other rental vehicles offered by our preferred rental suppliers.

Specialty vehicle classes, denoted by an asterisk (\*), are not offered to customers at the daily rental rates. Customers who wish to rent a specialty vehicle may be required to pay additional charges which will not be covered by ICBC. Examples of specialty vehicles include Mini Cooper, Smart ForTwo, and BMW 7 Series.
