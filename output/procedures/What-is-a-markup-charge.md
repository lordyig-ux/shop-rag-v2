# Markup charge

Definition of a markup charge relating to sublet towing services

### Definition

A markup charge is a percentage, up to 15%, applied to sublet towing services. Markup charges are

* allowed only on services completed by a company or employee other than the towing company originally called to the crash location, and
* calculated on the net amount of the invoice provided by the sublet provider.

### Use

The markup percentage is intended to cover

* administration costs for contacting and arranging services or additional equipment
* advance payment for the cost of equipment or services
* account management and billing fees, and
* financial risks associated with collections.

### Exception of use

The markup costs are not applied when a trade discount has been provided.
