# Estimate/appraisal form terms

List of terms and definitions in the Estimate or Appraisal.

### Terms and definitions

The Estimate or Appraisal uses several terms to describe specific repair or replace related items.

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Original Equipment Manufacturer (Nw) | a part supplied by the original equipment manufacturer.  **Example**: Ford or Dodge. |
| Aftermarket (AM) | Aftermarket means any part other than OEM.  **Example**: Jobber or after -market parts. |
| Like Kind (LK) | Parts from a donor vehicle supplied by an auto recycler (auto wrecker). |
| Sublet | A dollar amount for a service not normally performed by the repair facility. Examples:  * a wheel alignment, which is done at a mechanical facility * interior cleaning * custom graphics * lock recoding  Note: Prices for each of these would be in the sublet column of the Estimate or Appraisal. |
| Repair Hours (RP) | The amount of time it takes to repair a panel or part on a vehicle. |
| Remove/Replace (RR) Hours | The amount of time it takes to remove the old part and replace it. |
