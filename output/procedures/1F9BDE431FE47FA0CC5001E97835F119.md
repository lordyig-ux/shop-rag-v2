# Flat laminated windshield pricing

The estimator calculates flat laminated windshield pricing that is not listed in the National Autoglass Specification (NAGS) pricing.

### Glass pricing

Flat laminated or flat glass windshields are commonly used in commercial trucks, buses, and motor homes. Flat glass is priced according to NAGS pricing less the current discount.

The flat glass not listed in the NAGS Calculator may be priced by calculating the exact square footage and multiplying by the cost per square foot as shown.

Note: Refer to the NAGS Catalogue to identify the correct size of glass.

| Glass type | Cost per square foot |
| --- | --- |
| Tinted glass | $23.05 |
| Clear glass | $20.80 |

### Example

The price of glass measuring 12.3 square feet varies depending on if the glass is tinted or clear.

| Glass type | Calculation | Glass price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| tinted | 12.3 x 23.05 | $283.52. |
| clear | 12.3 x 20.80 | $255.84. |
