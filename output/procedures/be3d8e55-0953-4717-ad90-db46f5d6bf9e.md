# Material damage vendor rates and allowance schedules

The rates schedules, material allowances, and fees for glass repair facilities, collision repair facilities, and commercial estimates are available on ICBC's Business Partners site.

The schedules are located on the [Business Partners](https://partners.icbc.com/material-damage/rate-schedule) site:

* [Commercial (Truck) Body and Mechanical rates](https://partners.icbc.com/assets/94UrCrr88vdA3M87OELRP/Commercial-Truck-Body-Mechanical-Rate.pdf)
* [Commercial Labour, Paint and Body Material rates](https://partners.icbc.com/assets/2965hksS3xyFegP99gHjpF/Commercial-Labour-Paint-Body-Material-Rates.pdf)
* [Commercial Parts and Sublet Mark-up rates](https://partners.icbc.com/assets/3YlbqoYldFz8CyHpKTNQxU/Commercial-Parts-Sublet-Mark-up-Rates.pdf)
