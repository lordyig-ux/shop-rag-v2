# Refinish operations allowances

List of refinish operations allowances

### Allowance details

Refinish allowance applies for commercial and heavy equipment operations. These allowances are subject to various requirements.

The following details apply to allowance for refinish operations:

| Operation | Allowance detail |
| --- | --- |
| Refinish operations | * Panel preparation on repaired or replaced parts is included in all paint times. * Used part clean-up allowances may be considered. If allowed, judgment time applies. |
| Complete refinish | For complete paint times   * all welded, riveted, and bonded panels are included, and * a deduction of 0.5 applies for overlap per bolt-on component (e.g., sun visors, roof fairings, tank fairings, etc.). |
| Partial refinish | On partial paint operations   * a deduction of 0.5 overlap applies on adjacent panels, and * no deduction applies for overlap on bolt-on components (e.g., sun visors, roof fairings, tank fairings, etc.). |
| Bolt-on component Inners | All bolt-on component paint times include inners. |
| Removal of parts for refinishing | Removal of parts for painting must be in accordance with existing industry standards. |
| Spot refinishing | Where spot painting is done, fair and reasonable time must be specified according to of the actual operation being performed. |
| Stripes and graphics | * Stripe and graphic operations are based on judgment time. * If sublet, ICBC is not responsible for charges in excess of fair market retail. |
