# Apply taxes to glass only claim at a glass repair program facility

Glass repair program participants apply taxes, such as GST and PST, if applicable, to a glass claim.

### When this applies

In certain instances, there are applicable taxes that the participant must enter into the Glass Web Express (GWE) system to process a glass claim.

### Procedure

1. Access GWE system.
2. Enter the vehicle information and claim details.
3. Determine whether the vehicle owner is a GST registrant.

   | If the vehicle owner … | Then … |
   | --- | --- |
   | is a GST registrant | * select: GST Registrant Yes, and * enter the percentage. |
   | * is a GST registrant, but * does not know their percentage allowance | * select: GST Registrant Yes, and * enter 100 percent. |
   | is not a GST registrant | select: GST Registrant No. |
4. Determine whether the vehicle owner has a Prorate (P) plated vehicle or is otherwise exempt from PST.

   | If the vehicle owner … | Then … |
   | --- | --- |
   | * has a Prorate (P) plated vehicle, or * is otherwise exempt from PST | select: PST Exempt Yes. |
   | does not have a Prorate (P) plated vehicle | select: PST Exempt No. |
5. Provide an invoice detailing the total cost of repairs, including, if applicable, GST or PST, or both, which the customer is responsible for, upon completion of the repairs.
