# Complete a vehicle teardown estimate

Steps for an estimator to complete an estimate on a vehicle teardown.

### When this applies

A vehicle has sustained damage limiting the ability to complete an estimate and a vehicle teardown is required to determine a total loss when the vehicle repairs have not been started.

### Procedure

1. Determine whether a vehicle teardown is required.

   | If … | Then … |
   | --- | --- |
   | yes | * erase all estimate lines * using the `TEARDOWN TO DETERMINE T/L` pre-store from the ICBC templates, add teardown labour time and other applicable operations, if required, and * review and approve the teardown estimate. |
   | no | create a note that indicates no teardown is required in ClaimCenter |

   Note: There are additional items that may be included in the vehicle teardown payment, such as
   * evacuation of the air condition (A/C) system when the cost was incurred to facilitate the vehicle teardown
   * performing unibody/frame measurements to set up on a four point anchoring system when approve by ICBC, and
   * initial pull time when the repairs have not been started, and the vehicle must be estimated using the `TEARDOWN TO DETERMINE T/L`Pre-store in Mitchell Cloud Estimating (MCE) at the sheet metal (SME) rate.
2. [Create reserves through the exposure](Topic.aspx?rootmap=ClaimCenter-user-guide&topic=CCUG-PD-ANR612 "Create a reserve directly through the exposure when a Reserve Worksheet is not applicable."). 
   1. Update Cost Type: select General Expenses.
   2. Update Cost Category: select Investigative.
   3. Enter the total dollar amount of the teardown estimate in the New Available Reserves.
   4. Click: Save.
