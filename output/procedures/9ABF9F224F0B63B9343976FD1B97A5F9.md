# Correct glass pricing

Glass repair program participants must manually correct glass pricing when Glass Web Express (GWE) does not display the correct part price.

### When this applies

When GWE does not display the correct part price, the repair estimate must be manually updated in GWE with an exception line entry by the participant.

### Procedure

1. Retrieve the glass claim in the GWE system.
2. Verify pricing for the date work is completed.

   | If the part price that applies is the ... | Then the participant ... |
   | --- | --- |
   | correct price | submits the Glass Replacement Invoice (CL14BX) electronically. |
   | incorrect price | * manually corrects the pricing by updating the part and pricing on the GWE glass estimate as an exception item, and * submits the CL14BX form through GWE and supporting documents and images to Vendor Document Image Application (VDIA). |
