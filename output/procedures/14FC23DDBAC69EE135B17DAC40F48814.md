# Additional collision repair program estimate policy

Collision repair program participants may require an additional estimate (suffix) when they identify repairs that must be performed at another facility.

Participants must submit a Repair Facility Support Request form - General Request - B sheet, other to initiate the creation of the additional estimate (suffix). This can be accessed through [Make a support request (partners.icbc.com)](https://www.icbc.com/partners/material-damage/collision/pages/make-support-request.aspx).- General Request - B sheet

Total loss teardowns must be included on the estimate that flagged the vehicle as a total loss.

Important: Participants must not request an additional suffix for total loss teardowns.

Back-order parts do not require an additional suffix. For more information refer to the [Parts](https://assets.ctfassets.net/ways6qm8tbfw/2NgmPKP5LdEwCPfTWbCP8F/2178e918c12c066c5db0a91c2d9cc63e/collision-program-guide.pdf) section of the Collision Repair Program guide.
