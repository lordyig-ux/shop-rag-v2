# Fire risk hazards for a vehicle

The list of indicators that determine fire risk hazards on a vehicle.

### Indicators

Fire risk can be determined by visually inspecting a vehicle.

Risk of fire is present

* where any of the following exist:
  + exposed or damaged wires
  + visible arcing
  + ignition in the ON position
  + lights that are permanently in the ON position, and
  + leaking fuels or A/C systems, or
* where the opinion of the Estimating Services manager or designate is that a risk of fire otherwise exists.
