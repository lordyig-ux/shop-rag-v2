# Tow service level requirements

A list of service level requirements that apply to vehicle tows.

### Tow destination and service standards

Towing timelines and service standards vary according to the following tow destinations:

* claim centre
* repair facility, and
* salvage facility.

### Claim centre destination

The following table outlines the service level requirements that apply to vehicle tows from a tow compound to a claim centre in the tow company's local service area.

| When the tow request is placed ... | Then the tow company must ... |
| --- | --- |
| during regular business hours | deliver a vehicle to a claim centre within 3.5 hours of the tow request. |
| 3.5 hours prior to closing time of local claim centre | not charge additional storage rates after midnight (00:00). |
| 3.5 hours after closing time of local claim centre | * charge additional storage rates after midnight, and * deliver the vehicle to the claim centre by 11:00 the next business day. |

### Repair facility destination

The following table outlines the service level requirements that apply to vehicle tows to a repair facility in the tow company's local service area.

| When the tow origin is from a ... | And the tow request is placed ... | Then the tow company must ... |
| --- | --- | --- |
| claim centre | before 14:00 in the afternoon | * pick up the vehicle within 2.5 hours of the tow request, and * deliver the vehicle before 16:30 the same business day. |
| claim centre | after 14:00 in the afternoon | * deliver the vehicle by 11:00 the next business day, and * notify repair facility that vehicle has been delivered. |
| tow compound | before 14:00 in the afternoon | * pick up the vehicle within 2.5 hours of the tow request * deliver the vehicle before 16:30 the same business day, and * not charge additional storage rates after midnight. |
| tow compound | after 14:00 in the afternoon | * deliver the vehicle to the claim centre by 11:00 the next business day * not charge additional storage rates after midnight, and * notify repair facility that vehicle has been delivered. |

### Salvage facility destination

The following table outlines the service level requirements that apply to vehicle tows to a salvage facility in the tow company's local service area.

| When the tow origin is from a ... | And ... | Then the tow company ... |
| --- | --- | --- |
| claim centre | * the tow request is placed 5.5 hours prior to the claim centre closing time, and * the salvage facility has or does not have 24-hour access | Must deliver the vehicle within 24 hours of the tow request. |
| tow compound | * the tow request is placed during regular business hours, and * the salvage facility has 24-hour access | * must deliver the vehicle within 24 hours of the tow request, and * cannot charge an additional day of storage after the date of request. |
| tow compound | * the tow request is placed during regular business hours, and * the salvage facility does not have 24-hour access | Must deliver the vehicle within 24 hours of the tow request. |
| tow compound | * the tow request is placed after 12:00 midday, and * the salvage facility does not have 24-hour access | Can charge an additional day of storage. |
