# Manage a heavy equipment or commercial vehicle claim

An auto claims specialist manages a heavy equipment or commercial vehicle claim.

### When this applies

When a heavy equipment or commercial vehicle has a claim open.

Commercial vehicles include

* trucks
* vans
* buses, and
* other vehicles used by businesses for transport, delivery, or other operational needs

Heavy equipment is defined as machinery more than 8,800 kg, including

* excavators
* bulldozers, and
* other specialized equipment used in construction, agriculture, or industrial settings

### Procedure

1. Review coverage.
   1. Check tractor rate class and territory confirming correct one is listed.
   2. Check trailer rate class and compatibility.
   3. Check driver's licence to confirm class validity.
2. Contact registered owner (RO) and driver.
   1. Obtain a statement regarding the loss details.
   2. Confirm the use of vehicle on the date of loss (DOL) .
   3. Confirm the residence of driver.
   4. Advise customer of loss of use coverage.
   5. Note the conversation with the customer on the claim.
3. Arrange repairs for material damage.
   1. Determine declared value.
   2. Determine if claim is made for one or multiple units. 

      Note: Each unit requires its own claim.
   3. Acquire repair facility information.
   4. Complete material damage notes on claim.
   5. Assign material damage portion to an estimator or appraiser.

      Note: Use the [EST - Commercial - DRV activity](CCUG-RF-AN12V0-est-commercial-drv.html "The activity is created to assess repairability for a driveable vehicle when the heavy equipment criteria is met based on claim attributes.") or [EST - Commercial - NDV activity](CCUG-RF-579BNK-est-commercial.html "The activity is created to assess repairability for a non-driveable vehicle when the heavy equipment criteria is met based on claim attributes.") and assign it to the [Commercial queue](CCUG-RF-69CK0C-estimating-assignment-cmcl-queue.html "Activities related to commercial vehicles such as assessing repairability.").
4. Open loss of use exposures for the maximum amount.
5. Settle the claims.
