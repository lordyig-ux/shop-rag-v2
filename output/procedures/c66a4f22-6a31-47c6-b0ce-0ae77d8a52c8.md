# Daily storage rate

Definition of daily storage rates for ICBC tow suppliers.

Daily storage rates for ICBC tow suppliers are rates that are based on the:

* zone where the tow company’s storage facility (yard or building) are located
* supplier business type, and
* paid as per the [Towing and Storage Rates Payment Schedule (CL332)](https://www.icbc.com/partners/material-damage/Pages/towing.aspx) form.

The tow supplier must invoice ICBC based on the closet storage facility (yard and/or building) from where the vehicle was picked up, and must provide the following information:

* account or facility number, and
* daily storage charges that:

  + start on the date the vehicle arrives at the storage facility (yard and/or building), and
  + end on the date that an ICBC tow request is received by the tow supplier that is subject to the Towing Service Time Schedule.
