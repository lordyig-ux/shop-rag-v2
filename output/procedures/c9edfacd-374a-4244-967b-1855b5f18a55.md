# Apply the environmental charges/electronic fees in the estimating system

Apply the environmental handling charges or electronic handling fees in Mitchell Cloud Estimating (MCE) for the purchase of specified products for the customer’s vehicle repairs.

### When this applies

Environmental handling charges apply on Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) supplied equipment. Electronic handling fees do not apply on OEM supplied electronic equipment.

### Procedure

1. Select the applicable standard pre-stored entry in MCE.

   | If it is an … | Then the … |
   | --- | --- |
   | environmental handling charge | ENVIRO Handling Charge displays in MCE. |
   | electronic handling fee | ELEC HANDLING FEE displays in MCE. |
2. Enter the amount of the environmental handling charge or electronic handling fee in the Sublet Repair price field.
3. Repeat step one and two for each charge or fee for the customer’s repairs.
