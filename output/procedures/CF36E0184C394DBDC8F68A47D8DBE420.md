# Initiate commercial repair estimate for a non-drivable vehicle

Step on initiating a commercial repair estimate for a non-drivable vehicle.

### When this applies

Initiating an repair estimate on a commercial vehicle which is non-drivable involves arranging towing, determining the best estimating strategy and creating the Manual Estimate.

### Procedure

1. Retrieve the assigned EST - Estimate Vehicle - Non-Drive activity from ClaimCenter.

   Note: These are assigned to the claim centre estimator or appraiser.
2. Review the following in ClaimCenter:

   * vehicle location
   * customer's choice of repair facility, and
   * vehicle incident details.

   Note: The customer may choose a Direct Truck Repair (DTR) facility, or a non-Direct Truck Repair (non-DTR) facility.
3. Determine the estimating strategy.

   | If the customer chooses ... | Then ... |
   | --- | --- |
   | DTR facility | authorize the facility to complete the repair estimate. |
   | non-DTR facility | ICBC completes the repair estimate. |
4. Perform the following, if vehicle needs to be towed:

   * create a Request Vehicle Tow activity
   * assign to the Tow Support Queue
   * identify the Location as 004-Commercial Claims, and
   * inform repair facility of pending vehicle arrival.
5. Create a Manual Estimate from ClaimCenter.

   Note: This is done when an ICBC estimator or appraiser completes the repair estimate.
6. Attend the location of the vehicle to complete the repair estimate.

   Note: This is done when an ICBC estimator or appraiser completes the repair estimate.
