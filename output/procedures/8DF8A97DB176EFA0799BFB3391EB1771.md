# Tow recovery scene photos

A brief explanation of providing and using scene photos.

### Supporting scene photos

When additional recovery services apply to a vehicle tow, the tow truck operator may be required to provide scene photos along with a written description to support the required services.

Supporting scene photos assist in reconciling charges and assist with prompt payment for a recovery service.

### Scene photo use

Scene photos are helpful in documenting and justifying the need for

* additional tow trucks and services in more complex vehicle recoveries
* heavy towing recovery, and
* Category III tows.
