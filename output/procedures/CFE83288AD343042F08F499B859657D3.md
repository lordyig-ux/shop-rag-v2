# ICBC supplier business types

List of ICBC suppliers who may qualify under specific business types.

### Business types

* Independent body facilities
* Dealer body facilities
* Heavy Equipment dealers
* Sound equipment facilities
* Glass facilities
* Towing companies
* Detailing facilities
* Tire facilities
* Motorcycle repair facilities
* Mechanical facilities
* Upholstery facilities
* Truck canopy facilities
* Locksmith facilities
* Air conditioning facilities
* Rental vehicle companies
* Frame repair facilities
* Parts facilities
* Brake and Muffler facilities
* Paint-less dent repair facilities
* Aluminum wheel repair facilities
* Radiator repair facilities
* Welding and machining facilities
* Recreation vehicle repair facilities
* Heavy Equipment – Independent facilities
* Heavy Equipment – Specialty facilities, and
* New car dealer mechanical facilities.

Note: ICBC investigates all supplier owner(s) applications to confirm whether there is any previous unethical or fraudulent activity associated with them. When there is any concern with the application submitted, ICBC is not under any obligation to accept the supplier to be an ICBC supplier.
