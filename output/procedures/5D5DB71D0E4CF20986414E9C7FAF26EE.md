# Standards of performance for repair facilities on radiator and air conditioner parts

Guidelines for repair facilities on radiator and air conditioner parts.

### Standards of performance

Repair facilities performing radiator and air conditioning operations must meet these and all general standards of performance:

| Requirement | Standard of performance |
| --- | --- |
| Labour rates | Repair facility requests for labour compensation must be reasonable. |

### Standards are not met

If a repair facility chooses to use a supplier or a radiator or air conditioning part that does not meet these standards of performance, the repair facility and radiator and air conditioning parts supplier assume all responsibility for the quality of the part and workmanship.

| When a repair facility uses a... | Then all responsibility for the quality of the part and workmanship is with the... |
| --- | --- |
| supplier that does not meet the standards | repair facility. |
| part that does not meet the standards | * repair facility and * parts supplier. |
