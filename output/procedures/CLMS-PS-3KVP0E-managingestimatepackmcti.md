# Managing estimate submission in Mitchell TruckMax

Estimates received from commercial collision repair participants are managed by estimators/appraisers through Mitchell WorkCenter and Mitchell Connect.

### When this applies

When estimates are submitted by commercial collision repair participants.

### Process

1. **Commercial collision repair facility**
   1. Commits estimate package for ICBC estimator/appraiser to review.

      Note: The estimate is routed to the last approving estimator/appraiser.
2. **Appraiser/Estimator**
   1. Reviews estimate, including artifacts and photos.
   2. Approves estimate.

   Note: The appraiser/estimator commits and sends the estimate package, if supplement to self was required and the changes to the estimate were completed by the ICBC appraiser/estimator.
3. **Collision repair facility**
   1. Receives most current approved version of the estimate, and any available supplement, if required.
