# Taxi refinish exceptions

Exceptions for taxi refinishing and blend allowances.

### Exceptions

Refinishing for taxis is generally paid at the same rates as for other vehicles, using Mitchell Cloud Estimating (MCE). Two exceptions relate to inner paint and blends, as follows:

| When this refinish is required ... | And the vehicle ... | Then ... |
| --- | --- | --- |
| inner paint | * is a factory colour * has been painted and warranted, or * is viewed at the repair facility | the refinish is allowed. |
| inner paint | is not  * a factory colour * warranted, and * viewed at the repair facility | the refinish is not allowed. |
| a blend | is painted factory colour | the blend is allowed. |
| a blend | is painted fleet (non-factory) colour | the blend is not allowed. |
