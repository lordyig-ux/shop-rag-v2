# Completing air conditioning operations in-house policy

Policy on air conditioning operations that are completed in-house.

### Eligibility

This policy applies to all repair facilities and their employees when an air conditioning evacuation and recharge operation occurs at a repair facility.

### Applies

The policy applies when a collision repair facility qualified technician completes the air conditioning evacuation and recharge operation at the repair facility.

### Policy

The following policies apply to air conditioning servicing:

| Requirement | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Comply with regulation | Repair facilities completing evacuations to and/or recharging vehicles must comply with   * all federal and provincial regulations, and * Environmental Code of Pra   ctice for Elimination of Fluorocarbon Emissions from Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Systems. |
| Use compliant equipment | Repair facilities completing evacuations must use equipment that meets the specifications required in the BC Ozone Depleting Substances and Other Halocarbons Regulation. |
| Approved person | Operators servicing motor vehicle air conditioning systems must be approved persons as required by the BC Ozone Depleting Substances and Other Halocarbons Regulation. |
| Provide proof | Repair facilities must provide ICBC with proof that they have the required equipment and certification. |
