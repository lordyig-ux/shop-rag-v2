# Parts in stock at a collision repair program facility

A collision repair program participant must document parts in stock used for ICBC claims.

### Description

When a participant has a part marked in stock that will be used in the repair of an ICBC claim, the participant must document the following.

| Documentation | What the participant must store |
| --- | --- |
| Image of the part | A digital image of the replacement part beside the part being replaced. |
| Proof of source | Supporting documentation showing the source of the part, such as the  * donor vehicle identification number (VIN) * vehicle registration number, or * original invoice if part of a kit, etc. |
