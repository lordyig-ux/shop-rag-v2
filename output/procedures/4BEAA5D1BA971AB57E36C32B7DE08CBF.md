# Cash settlement enquiry or request

Steps for responding to either a customer’s cash settlement material damage (MD) enquiry, or cash settlement MD request.

### When this applies

When a customer has made an enquiry into, or has requested a cash settlement.

### Procedure

1. Determine if customer has a cash settlement enquiry or cash settlement request.

   | If the customer has a ... | Then ... |
   | --- | --- |
   | cash settlement enquiry | advise the customer that they require a repair estimate. |
   | cash settlement request | proceed to step 2. |
2. Determine if the customer has had a repair estimate completed.

   | If the customer has ... | Then ... |
   | --- | --- |
   | had a repair estimate completed | 1. create the [EST - Cash Settlement activity](Topic.aspx?rootmap=ClaimCenter-user-guide&topic=CCUG-RF-42GK1T-est-cash-settlmnt-activity "The activity is created when the vehicle is either repairable or a total loss, however the customer prefers to cash settle for damages.") 2. assign to the Estimating Assignment queue, and 3. add a note to the description filed stating "Any applicable deductible must be applied before payment/reimbursement to the customer". |
   | not had a repair estimate completed | 1. complete [Add a Guided Photo Capture MOI](Topic.aspx?rootmap=ClaimCenter-user-guide&topic=CCUG-PD-E05173-add-guided-photo-capture-moi "A   must be created before a link is sent to a customer or claims staff, and a repair decision can be made for the loss vehicle."), and 2. create the [EST - Cash Settlement activity](Topic.aspx?rootmap=ClaimCenter-user-guide&topic=CCUG-RF-42GK1T-est-cash-settlmnt-activity "The activity is created when the vehicle is either repairable or a total loss, however the customer prefers to cash settle for damages."):    * assign to the Estimating Assignment queue, with no location code, and    * add a note to the description filed stating "Any applicable deductible must be applied before payment/reimbursement to the customer". |
