# Handle repair network claim with New Vehicle Replacement (NVR+) policy

Steps on handing a repair network claim with a New Vehicle Replacement Plus (NVR+) policy.

### When this applies

A New Vehicle Replacement Plus endorsements effectively lower the total loss threshold to 50% of the vehicle’s actual cash value (ACV). RepairCenter has identified an estimate that has a NVR+ endorsement in effect. This lowers the total loss threshold for a claim.

### Procedure

1. Compare repair costs with ACV of the vehicle.

   If the repair costs for the vehicle with NVR+ coverage approach 50% of ACV, contact your servicing Express Department for further instructions.
2. Complete the task.
