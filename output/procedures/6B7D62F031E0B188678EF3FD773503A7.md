# Exchange parts requirements

Requirements for exchange parts with similar kind and quality, freight and pricing.

### Similar kind and quality

Exchange parts must meet all requirements of similar kind and quality to be accepted on the Estimate or Appraisal.

Note: All bumpers must meet these quality standards.

| Requirement | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Bumper texture | * Exchange bumpers are supplied re-textured and do not require additional allowance. * All bumper texturing must be paint-based with additive specific to the particular make and model to ensure the closest match. |
| Bumper priming | * All exchange plastic and rubber bumpers must be fully primed. * Standard black plastic bumpers do not require additional refinishing time. |
| Bumper reinforcement bars | * All bumper reinforcement bars must be epoxy primed. * If standard black, the reinforcement bar must also be refinished. * If a reinforcement bar is refinished the same colour as the vehicle (other than black), an inner refinishing time is allowed to the repair facility. * Two stage refinishing is also allowed if the original rebar was clear-coated. |

### Freight requirements

The following freight requirements apply to exchange parts:

| Requirement | Standards |
| --- | --- |
| Free delivery | All Exchange Parts Suppliers will provide free delivery as follows if located:  * in the Lower Mainland - from Horseshoe Bay to Chilliwack, or * outside the Lower Mainland, within 80 kilometres of their facility. |
| Packaging fee | Exchange Parts Suppliers may charge a $25.00 packaging fee for bumper and reinforcement bar deliveries beyond the included delivery.  * One packaging fee is allowed per claim, per exchange part source. * When such costs are incurred, no markup is allowed by the repair facility. |

### Pricing requirements

The following pricing requirements apply to exchange parts:

| Requirement | Standards |
| --- | --- |
| Substituting Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) parts | The repair facility may substitute an OEM bumper cover for any pre-priced exchange plastic/rubber bumper cover, if  * the Manufacturer’s Suggested Retail Price (MSRP) is $200 .00 or less, and * no aftermarket bumper is available for a more competitive price. |
| Core condition | All exchange parts must be supplied on an exchange basis, regardless of the condition of the original core. |
