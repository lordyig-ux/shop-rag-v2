# Sound and communication equipment allowances

A list of payment allowances for sound and communication equipment with no proof of purchase or receipt.

### Maximum allowance and installation time

Payment allowance given for specific sound and communication equipment with no proof of purchase or receipt.

| Item | Maximum allowance | Installation time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Player/Control Unit | $125.00 | 0.5 hr |
| Speakers (per pair) | $40.00 | 0.5 hr |
| Subwoofer (each) | $40.00 | 0.5 hr |
| CB radios | $75.00 | 0.5 hr |
| Amps | $50.00 | 0.5 hr |
| Equalizers | $50.00 | 0.5 hr |
| Wiring adapters (permanently attached only) | N/A – covered only with proof of purchase on same receipt as stolen stereo. | N/A |
| One Compact Disc (CD) | $9.95 | N/A |
| One Digital Video Disc (DVD) | $19.95 | N/A |

### Digital images

Digital images provided by the customer are not an acceptable form of proof of purchase. However, digital images stored by ICBC on a previous claim are acceptable.
