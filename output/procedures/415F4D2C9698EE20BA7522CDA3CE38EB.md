# Settlement of a total loss on a recreational vehicle

The claims representative settles a total loss for a recreational vehicle.

### Settling a total loss

When settling a total loss on a recreational vehicle, the claims representative must

* complete a Total Loss Recreational Vehicle Worksheet (CL30E) on every total loss, and
* review applicable valuation resources to determine accurate actual cash value (ACV).

### Valuation resources

The claims representative must use one or more of the following resources to determine an ACV:

* Specialty Services department of the Total Loss valuation provider
* product dealers - requires a minimum of three quotes, or
* independent appraisers, with the manager’s approval.

### Applicable policy

ICBC Autoplan Optional Policy, Division 5, Sections 5.11 and 5.12
