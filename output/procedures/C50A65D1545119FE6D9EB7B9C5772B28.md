# Moulding allowance document requirements

ICBC pays the moulding allowance when requirements are met.

### Allowance

The moulding allowance is paid when the moulding is

* replaced, and
* priced below $101.00, which is the combined total of all mouldings required per piece of glass.

### Mouldings priced below $101.00

All moulding replacements priced below $101.00 require the following documents.

Note: These requirements apply to glass repair program participants.

| Moulding type | Required documents | Payment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) mouldings | Copy of the original OEM part invoice(s) | ICBC pays the moulding allowance. |
| Aftermarket mouldings | Copy of the supplier invoice Note: A photocopy of the moulding packing slip or invoice with the applicable claim number identified is acceptable when mouldings are ordered by the roll. |

### Moulding priced at $101.00 or more

All moulding replacements priced at $101.00 or more which is the combined total of mouldings per piece of glass, require the following documents.

Note: These requirements apply to glass repair program participants.

| Moulding type | Required documents | Payment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) mouldings | Copy of the original OEM part invoice(s). | ICBC pays the retail price indicated on the OEM part invoice. |
| Aftermarket mouldings | Copy of the supplier invoice. | ICBC pays the net costs as indicated on the invoice plus a 25% mark-up. |

### Missing documentation

When a claim is submitted for payment without the required documents, the part may be removed from the glass invoice and deducted from payment.
