# Commercial refinish times for cab and sleeper

List of commercial refinish times for cab and sleeper.

### Included items

A refinish time applies to each cab, sleeper, or section of each of these.

Cabs and sleepers include

* day cab
* sleeper 36 inch
* sleeper 48 inch, and
* sleeper 60 inch.

Refer to the [ICBC Heavy Equipment (Truck) Paint Chart](https://www.icbc.com/partners/material-damage/Documents/heavy-equipment-paint-chart.pdf) for applicable refinish times.
