# Commercial supplier labour rate requirements

Requirements for commercial supplier to qualify for heavy equipment labour rates and services.

### Rate

The commercial transport industry includes many different types of heavy equipment vehicles that take specialized knowledge and equipment. Suppliers must meet specific requirements qualify for the commercial/heavy equipment labour rates.

Repair facilities that specialize in repairing heavy equipment vehicles and their machinery require specialized tools, equipment, and facilities. This entitles them to a higher labour rate than facilities that may provide limited truck or commercial vehicle repairs using facilities that were designed mainly for repairing automobiles.

Important:
[Mgr Commercial Est Serv](https://thehub/Search/Pages/advancedpeopleresults.aspx#k=JobTitle%3A%22Mgr%20Commercial%20Est%20Serv%20%22) must approve the assignment of the Heavy Equipment Business Type in Vendor Master.

### Labour rate

The labour rate for heavy equipment suppliers takes into account many factors, such as

* the larger repair stall size
* the longer turnaround time to complete repairs, and
* the parts price structure.

### Core requirements

To qualify for the heavy equipment labour rate the facility must meet all the following criteria:

| Criteria | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| ICBC supplier | Commercial/Heavy Equipment suppliers must meet all the general ICBC supplier requirements. |
| Primary business | Primary business of the repair facility must be repairing or refinishing commercial transport vehicles. |
| Vehicle accommodation | Facility must be designed to accommodate repairs to Class 8 trucks or trailers without requiring partial disassembly. |
| Signage and advertising | Facility must have business signage and published advertising that indicates they perform repairs of Commercial Transport vehicles. |
| Facility business type | One of the following Supplier System – Facility Business Types must be assigned to the repair facility: (03) Heavy Equipment Dealer, (47) Heavy Equipment Independent, or (48) Heavy Equipment Specialty. |

### Service requirements

In addition to the above requirements, the repair facility must provide at least one of the following services:

| Service | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Mechanical and collision repairs | Full mechanical and collision repairs with complete service for all drive train components. |
| Refinishing | Refinishing capability, with a spray booth that:   * has a minimum size of 16' H x 40' L * is of a steel manufactured design, meeting   + all applicable Federal, Provincial, Municipal and Regional District laws, and   + all health, safety and environmental regulations, and * has an air make-up unit capable of providing thermostatically controlled heated air. |
| Frame repair | Full truck and trailer frame repair capability with a manufactured, drive-on, designed frame machine. The frame machine must be capable of all frame repair functions including multi-pull or push operations. |
