# Manage a tow request for a vehicle determined to be a total loss at a collision repair program facility

Tow requests for vehicles determined to be a total loss are managed by ICBC estimators and claims support assistants.

### When this applies

When a vehicle determined to be a total loss must be towed from a collision repair facility to ICBC salvage.

### Process

1. **ICBC estimator**
   1. Rejects the estimate assignment in Mitchell WorkCenter.
   2. Advises the collision repair facility of the status of the vehicle.
   3. Completes the

      * Total Loss Worksheet (CL30) form, and
      * Total Loss valuation.

      Note: Refer to [Complete and upload a Total Loss Worksheet](MLWC-PD-0414NJ-complete-and-upload-cl30.html "Save a completed Total Loss Worksheet (CL30) to a claim in .") for more information.
   4. Contacts the customer to inform them that the vehicle is a total loss and advise them of next steps, such as

      * arranging the removal of personal belongings and license plates, and
      * towing of the vehicle to ICBC salvage.
   5. [Confirms the total loss](CCUG-PD-CTL247.html "Steps on confirming a total loss.") by updating the Total Loss Tracking tab in the Vehicle Incident screen in ClaimCenter, selecting

      * Yes, and
      * 060 - Central Estimating Facility location for the Total Loss Assignment Location and Lien Search Assignment Location.

      Note: For rolling total losses, the Total Loss Assignment Location is 045 - Kelowna is selected.
   6. Updates the total loss action items for the claim in ClaimCenter, including

      * advising the customer of the total loss
      * updating the estimated salvage amount, and
      * indication of Total Loss Valuation received.
   7. Adds the vehicle items and non-vehicle items in the Location Tracking tab.
2. **Collision repair facility**
   1. Determines if the vehicle is ready to be towed.

      | If the vehicle is ... | Then the collision repair program participant ... |
      | --- | --- |
      | ready to be towed | completes and submits the Repair Facility Support Request form - Towing request. |
      | not ready to be towed | * contacts the customer to arrange collection of personal belongings * prepares vehicle for tow from facility as discussed with ICBC estimator, and * completes the Repair Facility Support Request form - Towing request, entering all pertinent details required to facilitate the tow. Note: The participant is required to submit the Repair Facility Support Request form to ICBC prior to moving a vehicle to a tow holding yard or ICBC salvage, if applicable. If the participant is also an off-site salvage yard, storage cannot be billed to ICBC if the Repair Facility Support Request form has not been sent. |
3. **Claims support assistant**
   1. Retrieves the [Repair Facility - Tow to Salvage activity](CCUG-RF-FG1RLP-repair-facility-tow-salvage.html "The activity is created when a repair facility submits a Total Loss Towing Request through the .").
   2. Sends tow request to tow company.
   3. Updates the Salvage tab to [Add salvage action details](CCUG-PD-ASD639.html "Record actions within the salvage process from the Salvage sub tab in the Estimating Decisions tab.") to Release Vehicle for Sale, if a completed Vehicle Ownership Transfer Agreement (CL96) is received.
