# Claims excluded from the collision repair program

Claims can be excluded from the collision repair program due to claim type or other coverage reasons.

### Excluded claim and damage types

These claim and damage types are excluded from the program:

* declared value vehicle
* oversize and heavy equipment (over 8800 Kg)
* smoke or fire
* taxi
* theft from vehicle involving stolen items insured by ICBC
* total theft claim
* vehicle with no paint or body damage
* some claims involving old damage that have not been repaired, and
* vehicles that have been identified as an obvious total loss.

### Excluded claims due to other coverage reasons

In addition to the certain types of claims excluded from the collision repair program, some claims may be excluded for other coverage reasons:

* Specified Perils claims where coverage is not applicable to cause of loss
* vehicles insured out of the province
* claims where the customer has no Collision coverage and the third party has not reported
* claims where the Own Damage coverage is with a private insurance company, and
* claims where the estimate must be held, such as due to debt.
