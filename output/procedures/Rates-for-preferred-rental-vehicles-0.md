# Rates for preferred rental vehicles

The claims representative should encourage customers to use the preferred rental vehicle provider at a location; however customers always have the choice.

### Rental vehicle rates for preferred suppliers

Most locations in B.C. have a preferred rental vehicle provider who has an agreement with ICBC to offer advantageous rates, benefits, and services in exchange for ICBC marketing their service to customers.

In most cases, the rates from the preferred provider for a location will be the best available to the customer, relative to the rental rates of non-preferred suppliers in that location. Even where it is not, the ICBC representative should encourage the customer to use the preferred supplier.

Rental vehicle rates in general depend on

* the region, and
* the vehicle class.

Rates for [Rental vehicles](https://www.icbc.com/partners/material-damage/Pages/rental-vehicles.aspx) are also available on our website.
