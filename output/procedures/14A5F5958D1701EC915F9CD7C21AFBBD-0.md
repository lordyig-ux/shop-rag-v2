# Claims amount limits

Summary of the claim amount limits for equipment and own damage claims.

### Limits on equipment

The ICBC Autoplan Optional Policy, Div.5 S.5.11(b) [Regulation S.122] limits claims for equipment as follows:

| Type of equipment | Claims limit |
| --- | --- |
| Sound and communication equipment | $1,000 |
| Other equipment | $5,000 |

### Limits on own damage claims

The ICBC AutoplanOptional Policy Div.8 S.5(5) [Regulation. S. 117] limits own damage claims. The maximum ICBC pays is the difference between the

* deductible, and
* cost to repair or replace.
