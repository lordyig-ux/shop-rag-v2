# Preferred rental vehicle provider by city

ICBC has a number of arrangements with certain rental vehicle providers throughout British Columbia (B.C.). The preferred provider for a location generally offers special benefits and features, as well as the most advantageous rates.

### Preferred vendor locations

The claims representative in a city should always suggest the preferred provider of that location to the customer.

To see who the preferred provider is in a given location, see [ICBC replacement vehicle providers and locations](https://www.icbc.com/partners/material-damage/Pages/rental-vehicles.aspx).
