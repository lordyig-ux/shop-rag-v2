# Standards of performance for repair facilities on Original Equipment Manufacturer surplus parts

Guidelines for standards of performance on Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) surplus parts for repair facilities.

### Standards of performance

All repair facilities using OEM surplus parts must meet all these standards of performance:

| Requirement | Standard of performance |
| --- | --- |
| Mitchell Cloud Estimating (MCE) parts search | The repair facility must search for OEM surplus parts based on the information provided by the MCE database. |
| Use of non-MCE suppliers | If the repair facility purchases a part from a supplier not listed on ICBC's MCE database, the repair facility assumes any and all warranty for the part. |
| Repair verification | The repair facility must verify with ICBC that the vehicle repairs will proceed prior to ordering any parts. |
