# Coverage for tow truck equipment

List of tow truck equipment that is covered under the tow truck’s Own Damage coverage.

### Tow truck equipment

Tow truck equipment that is included under the tow truck’s Own Damage coverage:

* crane
* sling,
* winch
* hitch,
* beacon (emergency lights), and
* all paint finishes including special lettering or sign painting.

### Safety equipment

Safety equipment that is included under the tow truck’s Own Damage coverage:

* one safety kit, that may contain
* warning flares
* traffic cones, flags or reflectors
* a fire extinguisher
* a flashlight
* a first aid kit, and
* an earthquake kit.

### Exclusions

Tow truck equipment that is not permanently attached and is not covered under the Own Damage coverage:

* dollies and jacks
* jumper batteries and cables
* portable air tanks
* slider L and other specialty bars
* snatch blocks
* scotch blocks, and
* magnetic tail-lights, wireless lights, and suction lights.
