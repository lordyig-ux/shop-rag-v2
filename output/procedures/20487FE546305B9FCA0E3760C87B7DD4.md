# Unsafe vehicles policy

ICBC’s policy on reporting unsafe vehicles is designed to help make BC’s roads safer for everyone.

### Eligibility

The unsafe vehicles policy applies to all vehicles being seen due to a claim.

### When it applies

The policy applies when, during an appointment or estimate, an estimator discovers a customer’s vehicle to be “unsafe” according to the specific criteria, due to non-claim related damage.

### Policy Detail

ICBC will provide notice to Commercial Vehicle Safety Enforcement (CVSE) when a potentially unsafe vehicle comes to ICBC’s attention as a result of a material damage claim.

### Rationale for policy

The policy is in place to

* assist in ensuring that vehicles meet provincial safety standards, and
* ultimately, reduce the risk of future crashes.
