# Manage heavy equipment non-supplier repair estimate

Steps on managing a repair estimate from a heavy equipment non-supplier repair facility.

### Prerequisites

The repairs cannot be performed by a heavy equipment repair facility that has an ICBC supplier number due to the diversity of the equipment requiring repairs.

### When this applies

Some specialty heavy equipment non-suppliers may bill ICBC directly, and are eligible for heavy equipment labour rates. This is an exception to the rule that repairers must have a supplier number.

### Procedure

1. Confirm with the Manager, Estimating Services or designate that the claim involves a heavy equipment vehicle.

   | If the claim... | Then... |
   | --- | --- |
   | is a heavy equipment claim | accept and process the invoice as usual. |
   | is not a heavy equipment claim | contact the customer and supplier to explain that the customer must  * be added to the Vendor Master to facilitate payment when the supplier is not identified on the claim * pay the repairer directly, and * submit the invoice for reimbursement. |
2. Discuss the claim with manager or designate, and note the claim file accordingly.

### Specialized repairs

Examples of specialized repairs include but are not limited to cranes, backhoes and concrete pumps.
