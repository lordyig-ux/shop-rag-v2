# Tire advance disposal fees and tread codes

Definition of tire advance disposal fees and tread codes.

The tire advance disposal fees (ADF) is a fee collected on new tire sales and reinforced by the Tire Stewardship British Columbia Association (TSBC). The TSBC operates and manages the provincial scrap tire recycling program.

### Exclusions from the ADF

The recycling regulation excludes tire types that are

* used for cycles, wheelchairs, or three wheeled motorized devices designed for the transportation of people with physical disabilities
* used for aircraft or wheelbarrow
* retail value of less than $30.00
* recapped and re-trend tires, and
* tread codes for C, E, G.L, or IND.

| Tread code letters | Definitions |
| --- | --- |
| C | Compactor |
| E | Earth moving |
| G | Grader |
| L | Loader |
| IND | Industrial |

### Tread codes

Tread codes are referred to as

* grader/loader,
* small-off the road, or
* industrial tires.

| Tread codes | Definitions |
| --- | --- |
| Small off the road tires | * Tires of truck type construction for off road applications without Department of Transportation (DOT) approval, and * conventional sizes smaller than 16 inches cross section and wide base sizes smaller than 20.5 inches cross section. |
| Large off the road tires | * Tires of truck type construction for off road applications without DOT approval, and * conventional sizes 16 inches and greater cross section, and wide base sizes of 20.5 inches and greater cross section. |
| Industrial tires | Identified with a sidewall marking of IND. |
